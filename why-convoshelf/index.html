ChatGPT folders extension: 7 reasons to try ConvoShelf

ConvoShelf is a free Chrome extension that adds folders, full-text search and export to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok, in one list. You can import a ChatGPT data export and search those chats on your computer. Here are 7 reasons to try it, each one a feature in the current version.

Add to Chrome, free

Free plan with no time limit. Pro is $4.99 once.

1. An AI chat organizer: folders for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok

Put a Claude chat and a ChatGPT chat in the same folder. Tag and pin across all four AI sites. More

2. Unlimited folders on the free plan

Pro adds subfolders three levels deep. Drag a chat from the sidebar onto a folder, or press + to file the open one. Works on chats inside ChatGPT Projects. More

3. Search the words inside your chats

Find the chat where you asked about sourdough by what was said, not only by its title. Search covers chats you have opened since installing (up to 3,000 chats; titles sooner) and any ChatGPT export you import (up to 20,000 imported chats; a very long imported chat keeps its most recent 400,000 characters). Official exports from Claude, Gemini or Grok cannot be imported. More

4. Export a ChatGPT conversation to Markdown or PDF, free

Export the open chat to Markdown or PDF on the free plan. Pro adds JSON and bulk export of a folder or every chat. More

5. A ChatGPT prompt library with 10 free saved prompts

Save prompts you reuse and drop them into the chat box with one click. The free plan keeps 10 (a chain of steps counts as one). ConvoShelf puts the text in the box; you press Send. More

6. It tells you when a site changes

A self-check runs on every visit and shows up in the popup, so a change on an AI site doesn't break things quietly. More

7. No account and no server

Your folders, tags and saved chat text live in your browser's local storage. ConvoShelf has no account and no server. More

What you can check for yourself

  • Version 1.2.0 is live on the Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons and Microsoft Edge Add-ons.
  • The extension only asks to run on chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com and grok.com. The Polar license check is an optional permission you approve only if you buy Pro, and it sends the key, never your chats.
  • There is no ConvoShelf account and no ConvoShelf server. Delete everything from the options page with Delete all ConvoShelf data.

What makes it different

Most chat organizers work on one AI site. ConvoShelf puts ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok chats in the same folders and the same search, and folders are unlimited on the free plan. Compare options.

Questions

Which AI sites does ConvoShelf work with?

ChatGPT (including Projects), Claude, Gemini and Grok.

Is ConvoShelf free?

Yes. The free plan has unlimited top-level folders (creating new subfolders is Pro; any you already have stay usable), full-text search of chats you have opened since installing (up to 3,000 chats; titles sooner) plus any ChatGPT export you import (up to 20,000 imported chats; a very long imported chat keeps its most recent 400,000 characters), export of the open chat to Markdown or PDF, and 10 saved prompts (a chain counts as one), with no time limit. The free plan also includes a command palette and blanks in prompts. ConvoShelf never presses Send.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a single $4.99 payment through Polar, with no renewal.

Where are my chats stored?

In your browser's local storage. ConvoShelf has no account and no server.

Is ConvoShelf made by OpenAI?

No. ConvoShelf is made by Toolshed and is not affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or xAI.
