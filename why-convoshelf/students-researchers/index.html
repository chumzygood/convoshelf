Questions

Which AI sites does ConvoShelf work with?

ChatGPT (including Projects), Claude, Gemini and Grok.

Is ConvoShelf free?

Yes. The free plan has unlimited top-level folders (creating new subfolders is Pro; any you already have stay usable), full-text search of chats you have opened or indexed (no chat-count cap; titles sooner) plus any ChatGPT or Claude export you import (up to 20,000 imported chats; a very long imported chat keeps its most recent 400,000 characters), export of the open chat to Markdown or PDF, and 10 saved prompts (a chain counts as one), with no time limit. The free plan also includes a command palette and blanks in prompts. ConvoShelf never presses Send.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a single $4.99 payment through Polar, with no renewal.

Where are my chats stored?

In your browser's local storage. ConvoShelf has no account and no server.

Is ConvoShelf made by OpenAI?

No. ConvoShelf is made by Toolshed and is not affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or xAI.
