ChatGPT account deactivated? Appeal it and save your chats

If your ChatGPT account has been deactivated or banned, use the appeal link in the email OpenAI sent you, and try to get a copy of your chats out through ChatGPT's export or the Privacy Portal, because OpenAI says an appeal does not guarantee the account comes back.

OpenAI rewrote its help page on account warnings, suspensions and closures on 15 November 2026, and we re-read it that day. This guide follows it: which kind of action you have, how to appeal, what to put in the appeal, and how to avoid losing your chat history next time.

Disclosure: ConvoShelf is our product. We can't change or reverse any OpenAI decision, and this page is not legal or account advice. Only OpenAI's own notice and Help Center tell you what applies to your account.

First, work out which kind of action you have

OpenAI's page, OpenAI account warnings, safety suspensions, and closures, separates four things:

  • Warning. It says activity on your account may conflict with the Usage Policies or Terms of Use. A warning does not by itself mean the account is suspended or closed. Continued violations may lead to further restrictions or permanent closure.
  • Temporary safety suspension. Access is limited for a period of time and, in OpenAI's words, this does not by itself mean the account was permanently closed. Follow the instructions in the suspension email.
  • Account closure. A permanent close for policy or Terms violations or repeated misuse. The reason and next steps are in the notice.
  • Security or verification restriction. Access can be restricted if someone else may have used your account, or if you did not finish a required identity or age verification. In the verification case the account may be deactivated or disabled until you meet the requirement.

Check your inbox and spam folder for the notice before anything else. It names the reason and says which of these you have.

How to appeal

  1. Open the notice from OpenAI and use the appeal link in it. If you got separate notices for a suspension and another action, use the link for the decision you want reviewed.
  2. If you can't reach that email, OpenAI's page says to use its appeal intake form (for a warning) or account closure appeal form (for a permanent closure).
  3. If you lost access and never received an email, contact OpenAI Support through the Help Center.
  4. If you suspect someone else got into your account, change your password and rotate any exposed API keys, as OpenAI's page advises.
  5. For a suspension, OpenAI says you can email support@openai.com for account support, including refund or cancellation questions.

OpenAI states that requesting a review does not guarantee your account will be restored. Plan for both outcomes.

What to put in the appeal

For a warning or permanent closure, OpenAI asks for the details that apply to you:

  • Your user ID and organization ID, if available.
  • Information about your account activity and why you believe the action was a mistake.
  • The date range of any suspected unauthorized access.
  • Steps you have taken to secure your account or fix the issue.

If you see charges you don't recognize, add the date and amount of each charge, the last 4 digits of the card, the card brand, the issuing bank and the billing postal code. OpenAI says not to include the full card number or security code.

Getting your chats out when you can't sign in

OpenAI's help article Exporting your ChatGPT history and data, also re-read on 15 November 2026, says the export in ChatGPT settings (Settings, then Data controls, then Export) is not available while you are logged out. It also says to download a settings export while signed in to the account that requested it, and that exports can take up to 7 days to arrive.

For people who can't sign in, the same article says to request your data through the Privacy Portal: select Make a Privacy Request, then I have a consumer ChatGPT account, then Download my data, and follow the instructions. It notes you may need to verify account ownership through the email or phone number on the account. We haven't tried this on a deactivated account, so we can't tell you what comes back or how long it takes. ChatGPT Business, Enterprise and Healthcare workspaces have no self-service export; contact your workspace owner.

For the normal signed-in steps, see how to export a ChatGPT conversation to a file. If you can still open ChatGPT today, run the export now, because an export can't restore chats that were already deleted and may not be possible later.

Keep your own copy before something goes wrong

The export is the complete record, but it arrives slowly and only from a working account. For the chats you rely on, a second copy on your own computer is cheap insurance. We checked the following against the code of ConvoShelf 1.1.0, then the version on the Chrome Web Store, on 14 November 2026. The Chrome Web Store now lists 1.2.0 (updated November 18, 2026).

  • Saved as you go. When you open a chat on ChatGPT with ConvoShelf installed, it saves that chat's text into Chrome's local extension storage. Chats you never open are not saved.
  • One chat as a file, free. The Export tab saves the open chat as Markdown or PDF. This needs the chat open on ChatGPT, so it only works while you can still sign in.
  • Everything saved, in one file. ConvoShelf's settings page has a Download backup button that writes your folders and saved chat text to a JSON file. That page belongs to the extension, so it opens in Chrome without needing ChatGPT to load.

Saved chats are also searchable from ConvoShelf, see search all your ChatGPT and Claude chats in one place. The same habit helps with other AI accounts, as in how to back up your Claude chats before a suspension.

ConvoShelf has no account and no server, so nothing is uploaded. It also can't see chats on OpenAI's side that you never opened, and it can't bring back a closed account.

FAQ

Why was my ChatGPT account deactivated?

OpenAI's help page says accounts can be warned, suspended or closed for policy or Terms of Use violations, for repeated misuse after a warning, for suspected unauthorized access, or for not completing a required identity or age verification. The notice OpenAI sent you gives the reason and next steps, so start there.

How do I appeal a ChatGPT account ban?

Use the appeal link in the notification email from OpenAI. If you can't open that email, OpenAI's help page points to an appeal intake form or account closure appeal form. If you lost access but never got an email, contact OpenAI Support through its Help Center. OpenAI says a review does not guarantee your account will be restored.

Can I export my chats if I can't sign in to ChatGPT?

The export button in ChatGPT settings is not available while you are logged out. OpenAI's export help says that if you cannot sign in, you should request your data through its Privacy Portal: Make a Privacy Request, then I have a consumer ChatGPT account, then Download my data. We have not tested that route on a deactivated account, so we can't say what it returns.

What information should I include in an appeal?

OpenAI lists: your user ID and organization ID if available, what you know about the account activity and why you think the action was a mistake, the date range of any suspected unauthorized access, and the steps you took to secure the account. For charges you don't recognize, add the date and amount of each, the card's last 4 digits, brand, issuing bank and billing postal code, but never the full card number or security code.

Can ConvoShelf get my chats back after my account is closed?

No. ConvoShelf only saves the text of chats you opened while the extension was installed, in your own browser. It can't pull chats from OpenAI, so it can't recover anything you never opened. What it can do is keep the saved copies on your computer, where they stay readable if you lose access to the account.
