How to continue a ChatGPT conversation in Claude

Published November 18, 2026 · 4-minute read

Claude can't read your ChatGPT history, so to continue a conversation there you paste the context into a new Claude chat yourself: the goal, the key answers and the last few turns, either copied by hand or packed for you by a tool such as ConvoShelf's Handoff.

Disclosure: ConvoShelf is our product. The Claude facts come from the Claude Help Center, read on November 18, 2026. The ConvoShelf facts come from the code of version 1.2.0, the version the Chrome Web Store lists, checked the same day.

What Claude's memory import moves

Claude has a built-in way to bring over what another AI knows about you. Anthropic's help page says you run a prompt it provides in your current AI, then go to Settings > Your Claude (or Settings > Memory in the older layout), select Start import, paste the result and click Add to memory. It works on the Free, Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise plans, on the web and in Claude Desktop.

That import covers your preferences, personal details and recurring projects. It doesn't bring over a particular conversation. Anthropic also calls it experimental, says Claude "may not always successfully incorporate imported memories", and notes that Claude's memory focuses on work topics and may drop personal details unrelated to work.

For one conversation, the half-finished plan or the code you were debugging, you need to give Claude the context in the chat itself.

Do it by hand

  1. In ChatGPT, scroll to the top of the conversation and copy your first prompt. That's usually the clearest statement of the goal.
  2. Copy the answers you're keeping: the outline you agreed on, the code that works, the numbers you checked.
  3. Copy the last few turns, so Claude knows where you stopped.
  4. Open a new chat at claude.ai and start with a line like "Continue this conversation. Here is the context:", then paste the three parts in that order.
  5. Read it over before you send. Cut anything private you don't need Claude to see.

A long chat doesn't need to go over whole. The goal, the parts you're keeping and where you stopped are usually enough to pick up from.

Do it with ConvoShelf's Handoff

ConvoShelf saves the text of chats you open in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok in your browser. Its Handoff tab builds the same kind of context pack from a saved chat. In version 1.2.0 the pack contains, in this order:

  • the line "Continue this conversation. Here is the context:";
  • your first prompt, labelled as the goal (for some older ChatGPT saves it points to the saved context instead);
  • the chat title;
  • up to 10 passages you saved from that chat as clips;
  • the last 6, 12 or 24 turns, which you pick (12 is the default).

The whole pack is kept to about 12,000 characters, trimming the oldest of the recent turns first.

FAQ

Can Claude see my ChatGPT conversations?

No. ChatGPT and Claude keep separate histories. To continue a conversation in Claude, paste the context into a new Claude chat, by hand or with a tool such as ConvoShelf's Handoff.

Does Claude's memory import bring over my ChatGPT chats?

No. Anthropic's import copies what another AI remembers about you, such as preferences and projects, into Claude's memory. It doesn't move individual conversations, and Anthropic calls it experimental.

What should I paste into Claude to continue a ChatGPT chat?

Your first prompt as the goal, the answers you're keeping, and the last few turns, starting with a line such as "Continue this conversation. Here is the context:". Read it over and remove anything private before you send.

Is ConvoShelf's Handoff free?

The free plan copies one handoff pack per day, counted in UTC, for you to paste. Pro, $4.99 once, removes the limit and can open Claude in a new tab with the pack already in the empty message box.

Does ConvoShelf send the message to Claude for me?

No. ConvoShelf copies the pack or puts it in the message box, and you press Send yourself. If the box already has text, it leaves your draft alone.
