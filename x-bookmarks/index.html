Search and sort your X bookmarks.

X lets you bookmark a post but gives you no search, and bookmark folders need Premium. X Bookmarks Shelf saves each bookmark you scroll past into a library in your browser, with folders, tags and full-text search.

What it does

Full-text search
Type “postgres” and get the tip you saved in March. Searches post text, author, tags and your notes.
Folders, no Premium
Unlimited folders and subfolders, tags and pins. Nothing to do with X's paid folders.
Keeps deleted posts
If the author deletes a post, your copy stays, marked “kept”.
Export
Copy any post as Markdown, free. Pro exports everything to Markdown, Obsidian/Notion Markdown, CSV, JSON or HTML.
Bulk un-bookmark
Pro: tick 200 posts and remove them from X's bookmarks, one every 1.5 seconds.
Capture all
Press Capture all and it scrolls your bookmarks to the end for you.

FAQ

Does X Bookmarks Shelf need my X password or an API key?

No. It reads the bookmarks page you already have open in Chrome. No login, no API key, no server.

Where are my bookmarks stored?

In your browser's local storage on your computer. Nothing is uploaded. You can download a JSON backup any time.

Can I export my X bookmarks to CSV or Notion?

Yes, with Pro ($4.99 once): Markdown, Obsidian/Notion-ready Markdown with front matter, CSV, JSON and HTML.

What happens when a bookmarked post is deleted?

Your saved copy stays in the library, marked “kept”.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a single $4.99 payment.
