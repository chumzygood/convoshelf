Search and clean up YouTube Watch Later.

Watch Later has no search, no filters and no way to remove more than one video at a time. Watch Later Shelf indexes the list as you scroll, then lets you search it, tag it and clear out what you've watched.

What it does

Search Watch Later

Filters
Length, channel, watched, started, Shorts and unavailable videos. Filters are Pro.
Remove watched
Pro: filter Show → Watched, Select all, Remove. No monthly removal cap.
Duplicate finder
Pro: the same video saved twice shows up as a pair.
Folders on top
Unlimited local folders and tags on top of YouTube's list. Pro makes one per channel.
Export
Copy a video link as Markdown, free. Pro exports CSV, JSON, Markdown or HTML.

FAQ

How do I search my Watch Later list?

Install Watch Later Shelf, open youtube.com/playlist?list=WL, press Capture all in the 🎞 panel, then use the Search tab.

How do I delete all watched videos from Watch Later?

With Pro: in Search, set Show to Watched, press Select all, then Remove from playlist. Videos go one at a time, about 1.3 seconds each.

Is there a removal limit?

No. Pro has no monthly cap.

Does it work on other playlists?

Yes, any playlist page you can open.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a single $4.99 payment.
