Character.AI chats up for auction Dec 1: export or delete yours

Published November 27, 2026 · 5-minute read

Character.AI plans to auction its platform and the chat histories of more than 21 million US accounts on December 1, 2026; you can request a copy of your data and delete your account from Profile Settings, Account, Manage Account & Data, but Character.AI's export takes 3 to 4 weeks, and none of the sources we read says whether deleting now keeps your chats out of the sale.

Disclosure: we make ConvoShelf, a browser extension for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok chats. It does not work on Character.AI. The facts below come from the California and Colorado attorneys general's press releases of November 24 and 25, the states' court filing, and Character.AI's own help center and privacy policy, all read November 27, 2026. We don't predict what the court will decide.

What is being sold

Character.AI's parent company, Character Technologies, is in Chapter 11 bankruptcy in Delaware. According to California Attorney General Rob Bonta's release:

  • The sale covers the platform and the private chat histories of more than 21 million US accounts, more than 2.1 million of them registered to minors.
  • The chats go with users' personal data, including IP addresses and other metadata, and are set to be sold at auction on December 1, 2026.
  • Under the proposed terms, the buyer would not have to follow Character.AI's privacy policies or honor users' requests to opt out or delete their data, and could pass the data on to another company.
  • Character.AI's privacy policy told users, "we do not sell personal information for monetary consideration."

The only bidder named so far, Smirks and Pangs Acquisition LLC, was set up on November 13, 2026, one day before Character.AI picked it as the opening bid. The states' objection says it would pay $10 million in cash and lists the sale hearing for December 3 and 4. A coalition of 42 attorneys general, led by Colorado, California and Connecticut, is asking the court to reject the sale terms and, at a minimum, to leave out children's chats and other sensitive data.

Character.AI's privacy policy (effective July 1, 2026) does say it may disclose information "in connection with or during negotiations or closing of any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition." The states argue that bankruptcy doesn't override federal and state privacy law.

Step 1: request your Character.AI export

From Character.AI's help article How do I download my data? (updated October 8, 2026):

  1. On the web: open your Profile, click Profile Settings, then Account, then Manage Account & Data, then Export Data. A pop-up confirms you'll get an email.
  2. In the mobile app: open your Profile, tap the Settings icon, then Legal & Privacy, then Export Data. A "Success" banner appears.
  3. Watch the email address on your account. Character.AI says the export may take 3 to 4 weeks, and the download link is valid for 7 days from the time it's sent.

Do the math before you count on it: by Character.AI's own estimate, an export requested today arrives after the December 1 auction date. Request it anyway if you want a copy. While you wait, copy any conversation you can't lose into a document of your own.

Step 2, if you choose: delete your account

From How do I delete my account?: open your Profile, go to Profile Settings, Account, Manage Account & Data, and select Delete Account. The article says deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.

The privacy policy also lists a written route: depending on where you live, you may ask Character.AI to delete certain personal information or to opt out of sales, through support.character.ai/hc/en-us/requests/new or privacy@character.ai.

What we couldn't find out

  • Whether deleting now removes your chats from what is sold. Neither help article says, and neither attorney general release addresses it.
  • Whether a pending export survives deleting the account. The help center doesn't say. If you want the file, the safe order is to wait for it, download it, check your chats are in it, then delete.
  • What happens after December 1. That depends on the court. We'll update this page if the sale terms change.

So you're choosing between two things: a copy of your chats (wait for the export before deleting) or acting before the auction date with no promise it changes anything (delete now, without a copy unless you saved chats by hand).

FAQ

Is Character.AI selling my chats?

It is trying to. In its bankruptcy, Character.AI plans to auction its platform and the chat histories of more than 21 million US accounts, with IP addresses and other metadata, on December 1, 2026. A coalition of 42 attorneys general has asked the court to reject the sale terms. The court has not ruled yet.

How do I download my Character.AI chats?

On the web, go to Profile, Profile Settings, Account, Manage Account & Data, then Export Data. In the app: Profile, the Settings icon, Legal & Privacy, Export Data. Character.AI says the export can take 3 to 4 weeks, and the emailed download link works for 7 days.

How do I delete my Character.AI account?

On the web, go to Profile, Profile Settings, Account, Manage Account & Data, then Delete Account. Character.AI says deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.

Does deleting my account keep my chats out of the sale?

Character.AI's help pages don't say, and neither do the attorneys general's press releases. They do say that under the proposed terms the buyer would not have to honor users' requests to delete their data.

Can ConvoShelf save my Character.AI chats?

No. ConvoShelf works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok, not Character.AI. For Character.AI, use its own Export Data request.
