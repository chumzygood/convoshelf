Free

Tags and pins

Add tags to any chat and pin the chats you use most so they stay at the top.

Free

All AIs in one list

See your saved ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok chats in one list, with a filter for each AI.

Panel side and rail

Put the ConvoShelf panel on the left or right, or collapse it to a slim rail.

Auto-file rules

Search every saved chat

Full-text search across the chats you have opened, indexed or imported. It runs on your device.
Pro: filters by AI, folder, tag and date, and exact-message jumps.

Index my history

Index chats you have never opened in ConvoShelf, straight from your signed-in ChatGPT, Claude or Grok tab, with progress, Cancel, and resume where it stopped.

Pro: global search jumps to the exact message.

Clips
