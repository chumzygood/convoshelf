Available now

In the store today on Chrome, Firefox and Edge.

Free

Folders

Put chats from ChatGPT (including Projects), Claude, Gemini and Grok into your own folders, as many as you like.

Free

Tags and pins

Add tags to any chat and pin the chats you use most so they stay at the top.

Free

All AIs in one list

See your saved ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok chats in one list, with a filter for each AI.

Free

Search every saved chat

Full-text search across the chats you have opened, indexed or imported. It runs on your device.

Free

Prompt library

Save prompts you reuse and put one into the chat box with a click.

Free

Prompt chains and variables

Prompts with blanks like {{name}} or {{days|3|5}}, and chains of 2 to 12 steps that fill one step at a time.

Free

Command palette

Press Alt+Shift+K to search prompts, folders and actions from the keyboard.

Free

Export chats

Save the open chat as Markdown or PDF. Pro exports JSON, whole folders or everything, and a ZIP that keeps your folder tree.

Free

Handoff to another AI

Build a context pack (title, first prompt, recent turns and clips, up to 12,000 characters) to continue a chat in another AI.

Free

Clips

Save a selected part of an AI answer, then search it, relabel it or jump back to it.

Free

Chat outline

A list of every prompt you asked in the open chat. Click one to scroll there.

Free

Import your ChatGPT or Claude export

Read the official ChatGPT data export (ZIP or conversations.json) on your device and search all of it.

Free

Backup, restore and AI Toolbox import

Save a JSON backup of folders, tags, pins, prompts and clips, restore it on any browser, or import an AI Toolbox backup with a preview and Undo.

Free

Dark mode and keyboard shortcuts

The panel follows the site's light or dark theme, or you switch it by hand. Shortcuts open the panel and search.
