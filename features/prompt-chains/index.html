Prompt chains and variables

FreePro adds more

Prompts with blanks like {{name}} or {{days|3|5}}, and chains of 2 to 12 steps that fill one step at a time.

1617 second silent video with captions, recorded on a demo page with sample chats.

Why it helps

Reuse a workflow, not just a sentence, and fill in only what changes.

How to use it

  1. In Prompts, click + New prompt or + New chain.
  2. Write {{name}}, {{name:default}} or {{name|a|b}} where you want a blank.
  3. Click the prompt and fill in the blanks.
  4. For a chain, press Send, wait for the answer, then click Next step.

Free or Pro

A chain counts as one of the 10 free prompts. Pro is unlimited. See Pro.
