Chat outline

FreePro adds moreNew in 1.4.0

Lists prompts and answer headings of the loaded chat in reading order and marks where you are. Click a row to scroll there.

1313 second silent video with captions, recorded on a demo page with sample chats.

Why it helps

Long chats become easy to move around in.

How to use it

  1. Open a long chat and the Outline tab.
  2. Type in the box to filter the list.
  3. Click a prompt or heading to scroll to it. Scroll up to load older turns if needed.

Free or Pro

Free includes the outline. Pro adds a slim navigation rail. See Pro.
