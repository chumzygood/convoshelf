Tags and pins

Free

Add tags to any chat and pin the chats you use most so they stay at the top.

18 second silent video with captions, recorded on a demo page with sample chats.

Why it helps

One chat can belong to several topics, and your most-used chats are always one click away.

How to use it

  1. Open the chat you want to tag.
  2. In the Folders tab, click # Tag this chat, type a tag and press Enter.
  3. To pin, open All AIs and click the star next to a chat.
  4. Filter by tag with the Any tag menu.
