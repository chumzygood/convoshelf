Search every saved chat

Free

Full-text search across the chats you have opened, indexed or imported. It runs on your device.

14 second silent video with captions, recorded on a demo page with sample chats.

Why it helps

Find that answer from weeks ago by a word you remember, not by its title.

How to use it

  1. Open the panel and click Search (or press Alt+Shift+F).
  2. Type a word. Results show the date and highlight the match.
  3. Use "two words" for an exact phrase and -word to leave chats out.
  4. Click a result to open the chat.

Free or Pro

Pro adds filters by AI, folder, tag and date, and jumps to the exact message. See Pro.
