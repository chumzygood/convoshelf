Type // for prompts

FreeNew in 1.4.0

Type two slashes (//) at the start of a line or after a space to insert a saved prompt. You review it and press Send. You can turn this off in Options.

1213 second silent video with captions, recorded on a demo page with sample chats.

Included in ConvoShelf 1.4.0, on the Chrome Web Store as of __CS140_LIVE_DATE__.

Why it helps

Your prompt library right where you type.

How to use it

  1. Click in the AI's chat box.
  2. Type // at the start of a line or after a space, then a few letters of a prompt.
  3. Insert the prompt, fill any blanks, then press Send yourself. Nothing is sent for you.

Free or Pro

Included in the free plan. You can turn this off in Options. See Pro.
