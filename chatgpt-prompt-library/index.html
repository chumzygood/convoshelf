ChatGPT prompt library: save prompts and reuse them in one click

A prompt library is a saved list of the prompts you reuse, so you can drop one into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok without retyping it; ChatGPT has no list like that built in, and ConvoShelf adds one to all four sites, 10 prompts free or unlimited with Pro for $4.99 once.

If you paste the same instructions every day, this saves the paste. It also keeps a good prompt after the chat it came from is buried or deleted.

What ChatGPT already saves for you

ChatGPT has custom instructions. OpenAI's help page says they apply to all your chats, on every plan, and allow up to 1,500 characters on Free and Go and up to 5,000 on paid plans. They're good for standing preferences such as "keep answers short" or "I write in British English".

They aren't a library. Custom instructions are one block of text that applies to every chat, so a prompt you only want for some chats, like "turn these notes into a checklist", doesn't belong there. Projects in ChatGPT can carry their own instructions, which helps for one topic, but you still can't pick prompt number 3 from a list.

Three ways to keep a prompt library

  • A text file or note. Free and works everywhere, including your phone. You copy and paste each time.
  • Custom instructions or project instructions. Best for rules you want in every chat (or every chat in one project). One block, ChatGPT only.
  • A browser extension. The prompts sit next to the chat box and go in with a click. ConvoShelf is one; it is our product.

How to save prompts in ConvoShelf

  1. Install ConvoShelf from the Chrome Web Store and open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok.
  2. Open the ConvoShelf panel (Alt+Shift+S) and pick the Prompts tab.
  3. Click + New prompt, paste the prompt into Prompt text, and give it an optional Short name. Without a name, the first 40 characters are used.
  4. Click Save prompt. Next time, click its name and the text lands in the chat box. Nothing is sent until you press Enter.

Three prompts worth a slot:

  • Shorter email: "Rewrite this email shorter. Keep every date and number."
  • Error help: "Explain this error to me, then give the smallest fix."
  • Notes to tasks: "Turn these notes into a checklist."

FAQ

Does ChatGPT have a built-in prompt library?

Not as a list of saved prompts you pick from. ChatGPT has custom instructions, which OpenAI says apply to all chats, and Projects with their own instructions. To keep several separate prompts and choose one per chat, you need a text file, a note app or an extension.

How many prompts can I save in ConvoShelf for free?

10. The free plan shows a counter such as 3 of 10 prompts. ConvoShelf Pro, $4.99 once, removes the limit.

Does clicking a saved prompt send it?

No. ConvoShelf puts the prompt text into the chat box and stops. You can edit it, then press Enter yourself.

Do my saved prompts work in Claude and Gemini too?

Yes. The same list appears in the ConvoShelf panel on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok, because the prompts are saved in your browser and not in any one AI account.

Where are my saved prompts stored, and how do I back them up?

In your browser's local storage, in the Chrome profile where you saved them. There is no ConvoShelf account. The Download backup button in ConvoShelf's options writes one JSON file that includes your prompts, and Restore merges it back.
