After you pay: where the license key goes

  1. Polar emails a receipt to the address you used at checkout. The link in that email opens your purchase, where your license key is listed.
  2. In Chrome, click the ConvoShelf icon in the toolbar. If you don't see it, click the puzzle-piece icon and pin ConvoShelf.
  3. Paste the key into the Paste your license key box and press Confirm license.
  4. The first time, Chrome asks whether ConvoShelf may contact api.polar.sh to check the key. Allow it.
  5. The popup says "Pro is active on this device."

Thanks for buying. ConvoShelf re-checks the key about every 3 days. The check sends the key only, never your chats. Your chats stay in your browser: privacy policy.
