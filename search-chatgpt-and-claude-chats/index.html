How to search all your ChatGPT and Claude chats in one place

Updated November 18, 2026 · 6-minute read

ChatGPT and Claude each search only their own chats, so to search both in one place you need a tool that saves chats from both sites into one index, like the ConvoShelf Chrome extension. If you only use one AI, its built-in search is often enough, and this page explains what each one covers.

Disclosure: ConvoShelf is our product. The built-in searches below are free and worth trying first.

What ChatGPT's search covers

According to OpenAI's help center, ChatGPT's sidebar search is on every plan, on the web and in the iOS and Android apps. On the web you can open it with Ctrl+K on Windows or Cmd+K on a Mac. It finds chats, projects, images and documents in your account or current workspace, and it doesn't search the web.

  • It can match words in a chat's title or messages.
  • Archived chats show up in results. Deleted chats don't.
  • Locked chats don't appear in regular search. You find them under Projects, then Locked chats, with your PIN.
  • An old chat that has dropped out of the sidebar isn't necessarily gone. Search for a phrase from it.

What Claude's search covers

Claude has a chat list in the sidebar, and on paid plans (Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise) you can also ask Claude to search your past chats in a conversation. Anthropic's help center sets the scope of that search:

  • A search from outside a project covers all your chats outside projects.
  • A search from inside a project only covers that project's chats.
  • Incognito chats are never searched.
  • You can turn it off in Settings, under Your Claude (Memory in the older layout).

Free Claude accounts don't get this search-and-reference feature.

Why neither can search the other

Each search runs on its own company's servers against its own account. ChatGPT has no access to your Claude history, and Claude has none to ChatGPT. If you researched something in Claude and wrote it up in ChatGPT, you have to remember which site you used and search them one at a time.

The official data exports are a copy of your history, not a search box on their own. Claude's data export runs from Settings, then Privacy, on the web or Claude Desktop, and arrives as an emailed download link that expires after 24 hours. ChatGPT's account export also arrives by email. ConvoShelf can import the ChatGPT ZIP and make those chats searchable on your computer. It cannot import a Claude, Gemini or Grok export.

How ConvoShelf searches both

ConvoShelf is a Chrome extension that runs on chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com and grok.com. We read our own code before writing this section, so here is exactly how its search works:

  • What gets indexed. When you open a chat, ConvoShelf saves its title and message text to your browser. It re-reads the chat after you send a message so long answers are caught. Chats you only see in the sidebar are saved by title until you open them. For older ChatGPT chats, you can also import the official data-export ZIP in ConvoShelf's settings. ConvoShelf reads the file on your computer. Nothing is uploaded. Claude exports can be imported too; official exports from Gemini or Grok cannot.
  • How matching works. Every word you type has to appear in the chat's title or text. Title matches rank higher, and pinned chats get a small boost. You can search for an exact phrase, or use -word to leave chats out. You see up to 50 results, each with a snippet around the first match. Imported ChatGPT chats appear in the same list, with an Imported badge until you open them once.
  • Limits. Chats you have opened or indexed have no chat-count cap; your device's storage is the limit. For a very long opened chat it keeps the most recent 60,000 characters of text. Imported ChatGPT chats have a separate cap: up to 20,000 imported chats, and a very long imported chat keeps its most recent 400,000 characters.
  • Where it's stored. Opened-chat text and imported ChatGPT chats stay in your browser on your computer. There is no ConvoShelf account and no server.

Press Alt+Shift+F on any of the four sites to open search, or Alt+Shift+S to open the panel. The command palette (the ⌨ button, or Alt+Shift+K if your browser set that key) can start a search too. Search, including imported ChatGPT chats, is on the free plan. Pro, $4.99 once, adds filters by AI, folder, tag and date, for opened and imported chats.

For Claude, Gemini and Grok, chats from before you installed ConvoShelf aren't searchable until you open each one once. For ChatGPT, import the official export if you want older chats without opening them one by one.

FAQ

Can ConvoShelf also search Gemini and Grok chats?

Yes. ConvoShelf indexes the words in Gemini and Grok chats you open with it installed, in the same index as your ChatGPT and Claude chats, so one search box covers all four. Older Gemini and Grok chats become searchable after you open each one once, because only the official ChatGPT export can be imported. Search is on the free plan.

Can ChatGPT search my Claude chats?

No. ChatGPT's sidebar search covers your ChatGPT account or workspace: chats, projects, images and files. Claude's chat search covers Claude. Neither can see the other's history.

Does ChatGPT search the words inside a chat or only titles?

OpenAI's help center says search can match words in a chat's title or messages. Archived chats show up in results, deleted chats don't, and locked chats only show under Projects, Locked chats.

Can I search old Claude chats on the free plan?

Asking Claude to search and reference past chats is for paid plans only: Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise. On those plans, a search from inside a project stays in that project, and a search outside projects covers your chats outside projects.

Does ConvoShelf search chats I haven't opened?

For chats you open with ConvoShelf installed, it indexes the words when you open the chat. Chats you only see in the sidebar are saved by title. Open an old chat once and its text becomes searchable. For older ChatGPT chats, you can import the official data-export ZIP in ConvoShelf's settings instead of opening each chat. Claude exports can be imported too; official exports from Gemini or Grok cannot. Opened and indexed chats have no chat-count cap; imported ChatGPT and Claude chats are capped at 20,000, and a very long imported chat keeps its most recent 400,000 characters.

Is ConvoShelf search free?

Yes. Full-text search across all four AIs, and importing a ChatGPT export, are on the free plan. Pro, $4.99 once, adds filters by AI, folder, tag and date, for opened and imported chats.
