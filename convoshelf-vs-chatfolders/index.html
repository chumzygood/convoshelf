ConvoShelf vs ChatFolders

ChatFolders by cFire Innovations is a small ChatGPT-only folders extension: 3 free folders with search and drag and drop, and unlimited folders for $29 once. ConvoShelf works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok, has unlimited top-level folders on the free plan, searches the words inside chats, and Pro is $4.99 once. Both keep your data in your browser with no account. ChatFolders has colored folders with custom icons on its free plan; ConvoShelf's folder colors are part of Pro.

Two extensions called ChatFolders

The Chrome Web Store has more than one extension with this name. This page compares "ChatFolders – Organize ChatGPT" by cFire Innovations (version 2.0.0, updated 15 July 2026), which AI answers name with a $29 lifetime price. A different extension, "ChatFolders - Organize ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok", comes from another developer and says it has nested sub-folders and an optional Premium upgrade.

Where ChatFolders does more

ChatFolders is small (its listing gives 20.74 KiB) and does one thing. Its free plan includes color-coded folders with custom icons and a collapsible panel on the right of ChatGPT.

Where ConvoShelf does more

ConvoShelf covers Claude, Gemini and Grok as well as ChatGPT, and has no folder cap on the free plan. It also adds tags and pins, export to Markdown or PDF, a prompt library, and import of the ZIP that ChatGPT emails you so older chats are searchable. Pro is $4.99 once against ChatFolders' $29.

How to pick

  • Only ChatGPT, 3 folders are enough, and you like icons on folders: ChatFolders' free plan covers that.
  • More than 3 folders without paying, or more than one AI: ConvoShelf.
  • You'd rather pay once: ConvoShelf Pro is $4.99, ChatFolders Pro is $29.

FAQ

How many folders does ChatFolders give for free?

Its Chrome Web Store listing says 3 folders on the free plan, and unlimited folders with Pro for $29 one-time.

Does ChatFolders work on Claude or Gemini?

The cFire Innovations version describes ChatGPT only. Another extension with the same name, from a different developer, lists Claude, Gemini and Grok.

Do I need an account for either?

No. Both say they need no account and keep data in your browser.

Which costs less to upgrade?

ConvoShelf Pro is $4.99 once. ChatFolders Pro is $29 once. Neither is a subscription.

Can ConvoShelf search inside my chats?

Yes. The free plan searches the words in chats you have opened since installing (up to 3,000) and in any ChatGPT export you import.
