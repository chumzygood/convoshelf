Claude and Gemini chat folders: Projects, notebooks or an extension

Neither Claude nor Gemini has plain folders or tags for chats: Claude has Projects and Gemini has notebooks, which group chats around shared files and instructions, and if you want simple folders that hold Claude and Gemini chats side by side you need a browser extension.

This page explains what each built-in tool is for, where it stops, and when an extension is worth adding. The built-in tools are free and worth trying first.

Disclosure: ConvoShelf is our product. The Claude and Gemini details below come from Anthropic's and Google's own help pages, read on 17 November 2026. Both companies change these features often.

Claude: Projects

A Claude project groups chats under one name with its own instructions and a knowledge base of uploaded files. Every chat in the project can draw on those files. You can move an existing chat in from the dropdown next to its title, using Add to project, and archive a project when you're done with it (Anthropic: create and manage projects).

Anthropic says projects are available on free accounts, and that free users can create up to five (Anthropic: personalization features). There are no folders inside a project and no tags. A chat sits in one project or none.

Gemini: notebooks

Gemini's notebooks hold related chats, sources such as documents and PDFs, and instructions for that topic, and they sync with NotebookLM (Google blog: notebooks in Gemini). You create one from New notebook in the side panel at gemini.google.com.

Google's help page says some notebook features need History turned on. With History off, Gemini can't save new chats into a notebook and you can't add existing chats from the sidebar (Gemini Apps Help: notebooks). Google also says notebooks aren't available on accounts for users under 18 or on Workspace and Education accounts. Outside notebooks, Gemini gives you pins, rename and chat search. There are no folders or tags.

When the built-in tools are enough

  • You mostly use one of the two, and your chats fall into a handful of big topics.
  • You want the grouping to follow you to your phone.
  • You want the AI to read the same files in every chat on that topic. That is what Projects and notebooks are built for, and an extension can't do it.

When an extension helps

  • You use Claude and Gemini (or ChatGPT and Grok too), and one piece of work ends up split across sites.
  • You have hundreds of one-off chats that only need a home, not shared files.
  • You've hit Claude's five free projects, or you keep Gemini's History off and can't add old chats to a notebook.
  • You want to find a chat by what was said in it, across every site at once.

How ConvoShelf handles Claude and Gemini

ConvoShelf is a Chrome extension that adds a panel to claude.ai, gemini.google.com, chatgpt.com and grok.com. It only asks to run on those sites. Folders, tags and saved chat text live in your browser's local storage; there is no ConvoShelf account or server.

  1. Add ConvoShelf to Chrome and open claude.ai or gemini.google.com.
  2. Press Alt+Shift+S, or click the ConvoShelf icon, to open the panel.
  3. Click New folder, then drag a chat from the site's sidebar onto it, or press + to file the chat you have open.
  4. Switch to the other site. The same folders are there, so a Claude chat and a Gemini chat can sit together.
  5. Use search to find a chat by its words. For Claude and Gemini, search covers the text of chats you've opened since installing; titles show up sooner. Older ChatGPT chats can also be imported from ChatGPT's official export; official Claude or Gemini exports cannot.

Your Claude projects and Gemini notebooks stay as they are. You can file a chat that lives inside a project or notebook in a ConvoShelf folder too.

The free plan has unlimited top-level folders, tags and pins, search, export of the open chat to Markdown or PDF, and 10 saved prompts (a chain counts as one). Pro is $4.99 once and adds subfolders three levels deep, bulk and JSON export, and search filters. Creating new subfolders is Pro; any you already have stay usable.

FAQ

Does Claude have folders?

Not plain folders. Claude has Projects, which group chats with shared instructions and uploaded files. Anthropic's help center says free accounts can create up to five projects. There are no nested folders and no tags for chats.

Does Gemini have folders?

Not plain folders. Gemini has notebooks, which hold related chats, sources and instructions and sync with NotebookLM. Google's help page says adding existing chats to a notebook needs History turned on. There are no nested folders and no tags.

Can I put Claude and Gemini chats in the same folder?

Not with the built-in tools: Claude Projects only hold Claude chats and Gemini notebooks only hold Gemini chats. A browser extension such as ConvoShelf keeps its own folders in a panel on claude.ai, gemini.google.com, chatgpt.com and grok.com, so one folder can hold chats from all of them.

Is there a free Chrome extension for Claude and Gemini folders?

Yes. ConvoShelf's free plan has unlimited top-level folders on Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT and Grok, plus search inside chats you have opened since installing. For older ChatGPT chats you can import the official export; official Claude or Gemini exports cannot be imported. Creating new subfolders, and bulk export, are part of Pro, which is $4.99 once.

Do extension folders sync to my phone?

Not with ConvoShelf. Its folders live in the Chrome profile where you made them. Claude Projects and Gemini notebooks are part of your account, so they show up in the mobile apps too.
