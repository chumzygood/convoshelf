ChatGPT Pro 200 allowance drops after October 29: save your chats first

Updated October 28, 2026 · 4-minute read

If you kept the old ChatGPT Pro 200 usage allowance, OpenAI says it ends after October 29, 2026, and your $200 plan then moves to a lower allowance. If that has you planning to cancel or switch to another assistant, request your ChatGPT data export now, because OpenAI says exports can take up to 7 days to arrive.

Disclosure: ConvoShelf is our product. The plan details here come from OpenAI's About ChatGPT Pro tiers and Exporting your ChatGPT history and data help articles, which we read on October 28, 2026.

What changes on October 29

OpenAI now sells three monthly Pro plans: Pro 100 at $100, Pro 200 at $200 and a new Pro 500 at $500, which is the only one that includes Ultrafast. Pro 200 is open to new subscribers again, but new subscriptions that aren't eligible for grandfathering get a lower usage allowance than Pro 200 used to include. OpenAI's explanation is that its models have become more efficient.

Existing subscribers get a short grace period. According to OpenAI:

  • You qualify if you had an active Pro 200 subscription at any point from September 22, 2026 through 10 a.m. Pacific Time on September 29, 2026.
  • If you qualify, you keep the previous included allowance through October 29, 2026, while your subscription stays active.
  • After that date, your subscription moves to the lower allowance. The price stays $200 a month and the plan stays Pro 200. It doesn't upgrade you or add Ultrafast.
  • Only users affected by the change get an email with details.

OpenAI's help article doesn't say how much lower the new allowance is. It points to the pricing page for the comparison, so check there before you decide whether Pro 100, Pro 200 or Pro 500 fits how you use ChatGPT.

If you're thinking of canceling

OpenAI says a scheduled cancellation doesn't end your subscription right away. You keep access until the end of the current billing period, and you can undo the cancellation before then. Cancel on the platform where you subscribed: ChatGPT, Apple or Google Play. Deleting the app doesn't cancel anything.

Canceling Pro doesn't mean losing the ability to export. OpenAI lists data export from settings as available on Free, Go, Plus and Pro. It isn't available while you're logged out, or for Business, Enterprise or ChatGPT for Healthcare workspaces.

How to export your ChatGPT history

  1. Open settings. Sign in on the web, open your profile menu and select Settings.
  2. Request the export. Select Data controls, then Export under Export data, then Confirm export. The iOS and Android apps have the same option under Data controls.
  3. Wait for the message. ChatGPT sends an email or SMS when the export is ready. OpenAI says this can take up to 7 days, so check spam and promotions folders too.
  4. Download within 24 hours. Select Download data export while signed in to the same account. The link expires 24 hours after you receive it.
  5. Check the ZIP. It holds your chat history and other account data. Open it and make sure the conversations you need are there. OpenAI notes that an export can't bring back chats you already deleted.

If you can't sign in, OpenAI's Privacy Portal takes the same request: Make a Privacy Request, I have a consumer ChatGPT account, then Download my data.

FAQ

When does the ChatGPT Pro 200 allowance change?

OpenAI says grandfathered Pro 200 subscribers keep the previous included allowance through October 29, 2026. After that date the subscription moves to the lower included allowance, and the price stays $200 a month.

Who keeps the old Pro 200 allowance until October 29?

Anyone who had an active Pro 200 subscription at any point from September 22, 2026 through 10 a.m. Pacific Time on September 29, 2026, as long as the subscription is still active. OpenAI says only affected users get an email about it.

How much lower is the new Pro 200 allowance?

OpenAI's Pro tiers help article doesn't give a number. It says new and post-grandfathering Pro 200 subscriptions get a lower included allowance and points to the pricing page for the comparison between plans.

How long does a ChatGPT data export take?

OpenAI says exports can take up to 7 days to arrive by email or SMS, and the download link expires 24 hours after you receive it. Request it early and download it while signed in to the same account.

Can I keep my ChatGPT chats if I move to Claude?

Request OpenAI's export before you cancel. ConvoShelf, our Chrome extension, also saves the chats you open on chatgpt.com and claude.ai in your browser, and its free plan exports the open chat as Markdown or PDF.
