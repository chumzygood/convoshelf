ChatGPT Pro 200 allowance cut canceled: what changed on October 29

Updated October 30, 2026 · 4-minute read

OpenAI canceled the Pro 200 allowance cut it announced on September 29, so every Pro 200 subscriber now gets the higher Work and Codex allowance at the same $200 a month. If you were still planning to cancel or move to another assistant, request your ChatGPT data export first, because OpenAI says exports can take up to 7 days to arrive.

Disclosure: ConvoShelf is our product. The plan details here come from OpenAI's About ChatGPT Pro tiers help article, updated October 29, 2026, and its Exporting your ChatGPT history and data article. We read both on October 30, 2026. An earlier version of this page, from October 28, said grandfathered subscribers would drop to a lower allowance after October 29. OpenAI reversed that, and we rewrote the page.

What OpenAI changed

OpenAI's help article now says the reduction announced in its September 29 email is canceled. According to OpenAI:

  • If you already had the higher Work and Codex allowance on Pro 200, you keep it.
  • If you had the lower allowance, it has doubled to match the higher one.
  • On Pro 200, the lower weekly GPT-6 Pro message limit in Chat now matches the higher one.
  • New Pro 200 subscriptions are open again and include the increased allowance.
  • Existing subscribers get it automatically, without waiting for renewal and without canceling and resubscribing.
  • The price stays $200 a month and the plan stays Pro 200. Ultrafast is still available only on Pro 500.

Pro 100 and Pro 500 also now include twice their previous Work and Codex allowance, and they keep their current weekly GPT-6 Pro message limits. OpenAI says Plus usage is unchanged, and that a larger allowance doesn't guarantee twice as many completed tasks, because usage depends on the model, task and settings.

If you're still thinking of canceling

OpenAI says a scheduled cancellation doesn't end your subscription right away. You keep access until the end of the current billing period, and you can undo the cancellation before then. Cancel on the platform where you subscribed: ChatGPT, Apple or Google Play. Deleting the app doesn't cancel anything, and canceling doesn't refund earlier charges by itself.

Canceling Pro doesn't take away the ability to export. OpenAI lists data export from settings as available on Free, Go, Plus and Pro. It isn't available while you're logged out, or for Business, Enterprise or ChatGPT for Healthcare workspaces.

How to export your ChatGPT history

  1. Open settings. Sign in on the web, open your profile menu and select Settings.
  2. Request the export. Select Data controls, then Export under Export data, then Confirm export. The iOS and Android apps have the same option under Data controls.
  3. Wait for the message. ChatGPT sends an email or SMS when the export is ready. OpenAI says this can take up to 7 days, so check spam and promotions folders too.
  4. Download within 24 hours. Select Download data export while signed in to the same account. The link expires 24 hours after you receive it.
  5. Check the ZIP. It holds your chat history and other account data. Open it and make sure the conversations you need are there. OpenAI notes that an export can't bring back chats you already deleted.

If you can't sign in, OpenAI's Privacy Portal takes the same request: Make a Privacy Request, I have a consumer ChatGPT account, then Download my data.

FAQ

Did OpenAI cancel the ChatGPT Pro 200 allowance cut?

Yes. OpenAI's About ChatGPT Pro tiers help article, updated October 29, 2026, says the reduction announced in its September 29 email is canceled. Subscribers who had the higher Work and Codex allowance keep it, and the lower allowance doubled to match.

Do I need to cancel and resubscribe to get the higher allowance?

No. OpenAI says an active subscription gets the updated allowance automatically without waiting for renewal. If you cancel and later resubscribe, you get the same allowance as other Pro 200 subscribers.

Did the Pro 200 price or plan change?

No. The price stays $200 a month and the plan stays Pro 200. Ultrafast is still available only on Pro 500.

How long does a ChatGPT data export take?

OpenAI says exports can take up to 7 days to arrive by email or SMS, and the download link expires 24 hours after you receive it. Download it while signed in to the same account that requested it.

Can I keep my ChatGPT chats if I move to Claude?

Request OpenAI's export before you cancel. ConvoShelf, our Chrome extension, also saves the chats you open on chatgpt.com and claude.ai in your browser, and its free plan exports the open chat as Markdown or PDF.
