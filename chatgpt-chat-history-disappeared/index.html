ChatGPT chat history disappeared? What to check, and keep a copy

Published November 28, 2026 · 5-minute read

If your ChatGPT chat history disappeared, check that you're signed in to the right account and workspace, search the sidebar for a word from the chat, and look in Archived chats and Locked chats; a chat that was deleted can't be restored, by you or by OpenAI Support.

Disclosure: we make ConvoShelf, a browser extension for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok chats. The checks below come from two OpenAI Help Center articles, Finding your chats, projects, and files and Deleting and archiving chats, both updated in the last week and read on November 28, 2026.

Check these, in this order

  1. Account and workspace. OpenAI's first check is that you're in the correct account and workspace, with the same email address or single sign-on method. A work workspace and a personal account have separate histories.
  2. Search instead of scrolling. Open Search in the sidebar (Ctrl+K on Windows, Cmd+K on Mac) and type a word or phrase you remember. OpenAI says an older chat that no longer shows in the sidebar "has not necessarily been deleted", and search matches words in titles and messages. Try the All filter if Chats finds nothing.
  3. Archived chats. On the web, open Settings, then Archived chats. Archived chats leave the sidebar but stay in your account and still come up in search. Unarchive puts one back.
  4. Locked chats. These don't appear in regular search at all. Open Projects, then Locked chats, and enter your PIN or use device sign-in. Opening one doesn't move it back to your history.
  5. An outage. Check OpenAI Status, refresh, or sign out and back in. Recently created or deleted chats can take a while to update in search.

When the chat is there but replies are gone

If you edited a message, the replies that followed it can seem to vanish. They're in an earlier version: on a desktop browser, hover over the edited message and, if the clock icon appears, choose See versions, or use the arrows next to a count like 2/2. Copy anything you want to keep from there.

One other case: if ChatGPT ends a conversation over a serious concern about violence or harm to others, OpenAI says it no longer appears in your history or search, even though you didn't delete it.

What can't be brought back

  • A deleted chat leaves your account view immediately and is scheduled for permanent deletion from OpenAI's systems within 30 days, with exceptions for security or legal obligations.
  • OpenAI says you can't restore a deleted chat in ChatGPT or through Support, including when you used Delete all in Data controls.
  • Archive all keeps your chats; Delete all is permanent. They sit next to each other in Settings, Data controls, so read the button before you confirm.

If you write to OpenAI Support anyway, their article asks for your account email and sign-in method, the workspace name, the rough date of the missing chats, and the checks you already did.

Keep your own copy from now on

The only copy of your history that can't vanish from the sidebar is one you hold. OpenAI's own route is the data export: Settings, Data controls, Export data, and ChatGPT emails you a link to a ZIP file. See how to export a ChatGPT conversation and, if the file seems short, why a ChatGPT export ZIP can look incomplete.

ConvoShelf can read that ZIP in Options, under Search your older ChatGPT or Claude chats, without unpacking it and without uploading it. On November 28, 2026 we ran its import code (version 1.4.1, the Chrome Web Store version that day) on a small export file we wrote by hand in ChatGPT's format, to see what a reader of your export keeps:

  • Edited prompts: our trip-planning chat had a prompt edited from "Swap Sintra for Cascais" to "Swap Sintra for Évora". The import kept the version showing in the chat (4 messages, ending with the Évora answer) and left out the Cascais branch. If replies went missing after an edit, the export import won't bring them back either; use See versions in ChatGPT.
  • Behind-the-scenes steps: a web-search call inside the chat was dropped, so only your prompt and the visible answer were kept.
  • Archived chats: an archived chat in the file was imported like any other, with no archived label. Each chat keeps a link back to its chatgpt.com address.

After the import, press Alt+Shift+F on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok to search those chats. FAQ

Why did my ChatGPT chat history disappear?

Most often you're signed in to a different account or workspace, the chat was archived or locked, or it's simply too old to show in the sidebar list. OpenAI's help also lists an active incident, an edited message that hid later replies, and conversations ChatGPT ended for safety reasons.

Are old ChatGPT chats deleted when they leave the sidebar?

Not necessarily. OpenAI says an older chat that no longer shows in the sidebar has not necessarily been deleted; search for a word or phrase from it to find and reopen it.

Can I recover a deleted ChatGPT chat?

No. OpenAI says you can't restore a deleted chat in ChatGPT or through OpenAI Support, including after Delete all. Deleted chats leave your account view immediately and are scheduled for permanent deletion within 30 days.

Where are archived and locked chats in ChatGPT?

Archived chats are under Settings, Archived chats on the web (Data controls on mobile), and they still show up in search. Locked chats don't appear in regular search; open Projects, then Locked chats, and enter your PIN.

How can I keep my own copy of my ChatGPT chats?

Request a data export in Settings, Data controls, Export data. ConvoShelf, a browser extension, can read that ZIP on your computer and make the chats searchable, and it saves any open chat as Markdown or PDF for free.
