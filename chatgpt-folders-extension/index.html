ChatGPT folders extension: free limits and one-time prices compared

A ChatGPT folders extension adds folders, and usually search, to the list of chats in ChatGPT; of the three we checked on 16 November 2026, ConvoShelf gives unlimited free folders and charges $4.99 once for Pro, while AddonLoft's and NexaSphere's AI Chat Organizer allow 3 free folders and sell lifetime plans for $69 and $79.

This page compares what each free plan does and what paying once costs, using each product's own pricing page. Prices change, so check the linked page before you buy.

Disclosure: ConvoShelf is our product. We read the other two products' pages on 16 November 2026 and did not install them, so their rows describe what their makers say.

What ChatGPT already gives you

ChatGPT has Projects. OpenAI's help page says a project keeps related chats, files and instructions together, and that projects are available to signed-in users, subject to plan. A project is part of your account, so it follows you to other devices.

A folders extension works differently. It runs in your browser, adds its own folders on top of the chat list, and in the cases below can hold chats from Claude, Gemini or Grok in the same folder. You can use both: ConvoShelf works on chats inside ChatGPT Projects.

What the free plans include

  • AddonLoft AI Chat Organizer. Its pricing page lists 3 folders with drag and drop, title search and 3 saved prompts, across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok. Subfolders, full-text search, unlimited prompts and Markdown export are Pro. Every new install starts with 7 days of Pro, and the page lists a 14-day money-back guarantee on paid plans.
  • NexaSphere AI Chat Organizer. Its product page lists 3 folders, 5 tags, 10 searches a day, pins, Markdown export and 159 prompt templates, on ChatGPT and Claude only. Pro lifts the folder, tag and search limits. The page mentions a full refund within 14 days of purchase.
  • ConvoShelf. Unlimited folders, full-text search of saved chats, export of the open chat to Markdown or PDF, and 10 saved prompts, on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok.

What we checked in ConvoShelf

We read the code of ConvoShelf 1.1.0, the version on the Chrome Web Store, on 16 November 2026:

  • The free plan has no folder cap. Folders can nest up to 3 levels deep.
  • The free plan keeps 10 saved prompts; Pro keeps unlimited.
  • The extension only asks to run on chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com and grok.com. Checking a Pro license key with api.polar.sh is an optional permission you approve only if you buy.
  • Folders, tags and saved chat text live in your browser's local storage. There is no ConvoShelf account and no ConvoShelf server.

Pro is $4.99 once through Polar and adds bulk export of a folder or every chat, JSON export, search filters by AI, folder, tag and date, bulk move, tag and pin, folder colors and unlimited saved prompts. The full table is on the ConvoShelf Pro page.

How to pick

  • If you only use ChatGPT and want your grouping on every device, try ChatGPT's own Projects first. They cost nothing extra.
  • If you need more than 3 folders without paying, the free plans differ most here: ConvoShelf has no cap, the other two stop at 3.
  • If you use Gemini or Grok as well as ChatGPT, NexaSphere's extension doesn't cover them; ConvoShelf and AddonLoft's do.
  • If you'd rather not subscribe, all three sell a one-time option, at $4.99, $69 and $79.

FAQ

Does ChatGPT have folders built in?

ChatGPT has Projects, which OpenAI describes as a way to keep related chats, files and instructions together, available to signed-in users subject to plan. A folders extension adds folders and search on top of the chat list, and some work across Claude, Gemini and Grok too.

Is there a free ChatGPT folders extension?

Yes. All three we checked have a free plan with no time limit. ConvoShelf's free plan has unlimited folders. AddonLoft's AI Chat Organizer and NexaSphere's AI Chat Organizer allow 3 folders on their free plans.

Which ChatGPT folders extension can I buy once?

On 16 November 2026, ConvoShelf Pro was $4.99 once, AddonLoft's AI Chat Organizer had a $69 lifetime plan, and NexaSphere's AI Chat Organizer had a $79 lifetime plan. The last two also sell monthly and yearly plans.

Does a folders extension move or change my ChatGPT chats?

ConvoShelf doesn't. It keeps its folders, tags and saved chat text in your browser's local storage, and your chats stay where they are on ChatGPT. AddonLoft and NexaSphere also say their folders are stored in your browser.

Will my folders show up on my phone?

Not with ConvoShelf. Folders live in the Chrome profile where you made them. ChatGPT's own Projects are part of your account, and OpenAI says you can start a project on one device and continue on another.
