ChatGPT temporary chat: what it keeps, and how to save one

Published November 20, 2026 · 5-minute read

A ChatGPT temporary chat stays out of your chat history and isn't used to train OpenAI's models, but OpenAI may keep a copy for up to 30 days for safety, and you can save the chat to your history if you decide to keep it.

Disclosure: ConvoShelf is our product. The ChatGPT facts below come from OpenAI's Help Center articles Temporary chat in ChatGPT and Chat and file retention in ChatGPT, the Claude facts from Anthropic's Use incognito chats, and the Gemini facts from Google's Gemini Apps Privacy Hub and Use Gemini Apps help page, all read on November 20, 2026. The ConvoShelf facts come from the code of version 1.2.0, the version live on the Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons and Edge Add-ons.

What a temporary chat does and doesn't do

  • History: it stays out of your chat history unless you save it.
  • Training: it isn't used to improve OpenAI's models while it stays temporary.
  • Memory: it never creates or updates memories while temporary. It can read your existing memories, custom instructions and plugins only if you choose Personalized.
  • Retention: OpenAI may keep a copy for up to 30 days for safety purposes.
  • Safety context: OpenAI says ChatGPT may still use limited information from prior conversations for safety and security in rare, high-risk situations, even with memory and personalization off.

That 30-day copy is why a post on X on November 19, 2026 called incognito AI chats "a delayed delete." The chat leaves your screen right away, but OpenAI may hold a copy for up to a month.

How to start a temporary chat

  1. Open a new chat in ChatGPT.
  2. Select Temporary.
  3. Choose Personalized or Unpersonalized before you send your first message. You can't change it after the conversation starts.
  4. Type your message.

In the ChatGPT desktop app, OpenAI's Commands reference lists Ctrl+Shift+N on Windows and Cmd+Shift+N on a Mac for a new temporary chat. Our ChatGPT keyboard shortcuts page has the rest of the list.

Can you save a ChatGPT temporary chat?

Yes. OpenAI's Help Center says you can save a temporary chat to your chat history if you want to come back to it. Saving turns it into a regular chat, even if it started Unpersonalized. Three things change when you do:

  • It follows your account's model-improvement setting from then on. If that setting is on, the saved chat can be used to improve OpenAI's models.
  • If Memory is on, the saved chat may be used to personalize future responses.
  • It follows the retention rules for saved chats: it stays in your account until you delete it, and a deleted chat is scheduled for permanent deletion within 30 days, per OpenAI's retention article.

If the temporary chat had uploaded files, OpenAI says eligible files may also be saved to Library or Space when you save the chat.

The catch is timing. If you leave a temporary chat without saving it, it won't appear in your history and can't be reopened from history. There's no way to save it after you close it.

Keeping the chats you want to keep

A temporary chat is for the conversations you don't want to keep. For the ones you do, the order matters: decide before you leave the chat.

  1. Save it in ChatGPT first. That turns it into a regular chat with its own address, chatgpt.com/c/ followed by the chat's ID.
  2. File it. ConvoShelf puts saved chats from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok into your own folders in the browser. Top-level folders are unlimited on the free plan; subfolders are Pro. See how to organize ChatGPT chats into folders.
  3. Keep your own copy. ConvoShelf exports the open chat as Markdown or PDF on the free plan, so a copy lives on your computer even if the chat is later deleted. For every chat in your account at once, use OpenAI's official export, covered in how to export a ChatGPT conversation to a file.

We checked the 1.2.0 code, and ConvoShelf has no handling for temporary or incognito chats. It files and exports chats by their chat ID, and we haven't tested it inside an unsaved temporary chat, so we don't claim it can save one. If you're in a temporary chat and want any of it, save the chat in ChatGPT or copy the text out before you leave.

If you want a saved chat gone later, deleting it doesn't always remove what ChatGPT remembered from it. See does deleting a ChatGPT chat delete its memories.

FAQ

How long does ChatGPT keep a temporary chat?

OpenAI's Help Center says it may keep a copy of a temporary chat for up to 30 days for safety purposes. The chat doesn't appear in your history unless you save it, and once saved it follows the retention rules for regular chats.

Can I save a ChatGPT temporary chat?

Yes. OpenAI says you can save a temporary chat to your chat history, which turns it into a regular chat. From then on it follows your account's personalization and model-improvement settings.

Can I get a temporary chat back after I close it?

No. If you leave a temporary chat without saving it, OpenAI says it won't appear in your history and can't be reopened from history. Save it or copy what you need before you leave.

Does ChatGPT temporary chat use my memory?

Only if you pick Personalized before your first message. Unpersonalized doesn't use memory, custom instructions or plugins. Neither option creates or updates memories while the chat stays temporary.

Can ConvoShelf save a ChatGPT temporary chat?

We don't claim it can. ConvoShelf 1.2.0 files and exports chats by their chat ID, and we haven't tested it inside an unsaved temporary chat. Save the temporary chat in ChatGPT first; then it's a regular chat that ConvoShelf can put in a folder and export as Markdown or PDF.
