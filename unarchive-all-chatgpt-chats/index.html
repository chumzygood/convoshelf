No 'Unarchive all' in ChatGPT? How to restore archived chats

Published November 23, 2026 · 4-minute read

ChatGPT has an Archive all chats button but no Unarchive all: archived chats come back one at a time from Settings > Data controls > Archived chats, and nothing was deleted, so the practical fix is to search for the chats you need and unarchive only those.

Disclosure: ConvoShelf is our product. The ChatGPT facts below come from OpenAI's Help Center articles Deleting and archiving chats in ChatGPT (it showed "Updated: 17 hours ago" when we read it on November 23, 2026) and Exporting your ChatGPT history and data, and from OpenAI Support replies on the OpenAI community forum. The ConvoShelf facts come from the code of version 1.2.0, the version live on the Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons and Edge Add-ons.

What Archive all chats did

OpenAI's help page puts Archive all chats and Delete all chats side by side in Settings > Data controls. Archive all moves every chat out of your regular sidebar, and OpenAI says it can affect chats in your current account or workspace, including chats in projects. Three things from the same page are worth knowing before you do anything else:

  • Archiving doesn't delete. Archived chats stay saved to your account until you delete them, and archiving doesn't change their retention period.
  • Archived chats remain searchable.
  • Delete all chats can't be undone. If you're cleaning up after an accidental Archive all, stay away from the button next to it.

How to unarchive ChatGPT chats

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select Data controls.
  3. Select Archived chats or View archived chats, depending on your device. If Manage appears, select it.
  4. Select Unarchive next to a chat to return it to your regular history. Repeat for each chat.

There is no select-all, multi-select or bulk restore in that list. On November 8, 2026, OpenAI Support answered a user who had pressed Archive all by mistake: "Unfortunately, chats currently need to be unarchived individually," and said the request would go to the product team. Support gave similar answers to earlier requests on June 9 and August 6, 2026, with no timeline.

Restore the chats you need, not all of them

If you have hundreds of archived chats, clicking Unarchive hundreds of times is the only built-in route. Most people need a few dozen of them back. Two ways to find those first:

  • ChatGPT's own Search. OpenAI says archived chats remain searchable, so search for a word you remember and open the chat from there.
  • Your data export. In Settings > Data controls, under Export data, select Export and confirm. OpenAI says the ZIP can take up to 7 days to arrive, the email link expires 24 hours after you get it, and the ZIP includes your chat history with an index.html viewer that searches your conversations locally in the browser. OpenAI's page doesn't say whether archived chats are listed separately, so open the viewer and search for the title of a chat you know is archived to confirm it's there.

Once you know which chats matter, unarchive those and leave the rest archived. They are still saved and searchable. Keeping a searchable copy with ConvoShelf

ConvoShelf can't unarchive anything, and it doesn't change your ChatGPT account. What it can do, checked in the 1.2.0 code:

  • Import the export ZIP. On ConvoShelf's options page, the Import box takes the ZIP as ChatGPT sends it (or the conversations.json inside it). It is read on your computer, and nothing is uploaded.
  • It doesn't skip archived chats. The importer reads every conversation in the file and skips only empty ones. It has no archived filter, so whatever ChatGPT put in the export gets imported.
  • Search the words inside them. Imported chats appear in ConvoShelf search next to the chats you've opened since installing. Each result keeps the chat's chatgpt.com link.
  • Limits. Up to 20,000 imported chats. A very long chat keeps its most recent 400,000 characters. Import and search are on the free plan; search filters by AI, folder, tag and date are Pro ($4.99 once).

FAQ

Is there an Unarchive all button in ChatGPT?

No. OpenAI's Help Center lists Archive all chats under Settings > Data controls, but restoring is per chat: open Archived chats and select Unarchive on each one. On November 8, 2026 OpenAI Support wrote on its community forum that chats currently need to be unarchived individually.

Does Archive all chats delete my ChatGPT chats?

No. OpenAI says archiving removes chats from the regular sidebar without deleting them, and archived chats stay saved under the same retention rules as other chats until you delete them.

How do I find an archived chat without unarchiving everything?

OpenAI says archived chats remain searchable, so use ChatGPT's Search first. You can also open Settings > Data controls > Archived chats and unarchive only the chats you need.

Can I undo Delete all chats?

No. OpenAI says deleted chats, including chats removed with Delete all chats, cannot be restored, in ChatGPT or through support. Archive all and Delete all sit next to each other in Data controls, so read the confirmation before you continue.

Can ConvoShelf unarchive my ChatGPT chats?

No. ConvoShelf doesn't change anything in your ChatGPT account. It can import the export ZIP that ChatGPT emails you and make those chats searchable in your browser, which helps you find the ones worth unarchiving.
