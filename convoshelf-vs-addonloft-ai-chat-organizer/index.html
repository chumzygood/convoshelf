ConvoShelf vs AI Chat Organizer (AddonLoft)

ConvoShelf and AddonLoft's AI Chat Organizer do the same core job: folders, search and a prompt library across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok, stored in your browser with no account. The difference is the free plan and the price. ConvoShelf's free plan has unlimited folders and searches inside chats, and Pro is $4.99 once. AI Chat Organizer's free plan has 3 folders and title search, and Pro is $5 a month, $36 a year or $69 for life, with a 7-day trial.

Where AI Chat Organizer does more

AI Chat Organizer gives every new install 7 days of full Pro, so you can try subfolders and full-text search before paying. Its FAQ says site fixes are delivered remotely and reach installed copies within hours when an AI site changes its design. It also supports right-to-left languages and lets you keep the panel on the left or right.

Where ConvoShelf does more

ConvoShelf's free plan has no folder cap, searches the words inside your chats, and exports the open chat to Markdown or PDF. AI Chat Organizer keeps those behind Pro. ConvoShelf also runs on Firefox and can import the ZIP that ChatGPT emails you, so you can search chats from before you installed it. Pro costs $4.99 once.

How to pick

  • You want more than 3 folders without paying: ConvoShelf.
  • You want to try every paid feature for a week first: AI Chat Organizer's trial does that.
  • You use Firefox: ConvoShelf. AI Chat Organizer lists Chrome and Edge.
  • You'd rather pay once: ConvoShelf Pro is $4.99, and AI Chat Organizer's lifetime plan is $69.

FAQ

Which one has the better free plan?

For folders and search, ConvoShelf: unlimited top-level folders and search inside chats, against 3 folders and title search on AI Chat Organizer's free plan.

Do both work on Claude, Gemini and Grok?

Yes. Both list ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok, and both say one folder can hold chats from all four.

Is either one a subscription?

ConvoShelf Pro is $4.99 once with no subscription. AI Chat Organizer offers $5 a month or $36 a year, and also a $69 lifetime license.

Do they upload my chats?

Neither says it does. ConvoShelf keeps folders and saved chat text in your browser and only sends a Pro license key to Polar for checking. AddonLoft says its data is stored in your Chrome profile with no account and no cloud.

Can I try Pro features before buying?

AI Chat Organizer starts every new install with a 7-day Pro trial. ConvoShelf has no trial, but its free plan has no time limit.
