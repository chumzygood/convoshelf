Claude Sonnet 4.6 leaves the model selector on December 8: what happens to your chats

Published November 26, 2026 · 4-minute read · Leia em português

On December 8, 2026, Anthropic removes Claude Sonnet 4.6 from the model selector on the Free, Pro, Max and Ultra plans and recommends Claude Sonnet 5.5 instead. Your Sonnet 4.6 chats stay in your account and nothing is deleted. After December 8 they move to Sonnet 5.5 or your default model as you continue them, but Anthropic says not every chat or scheduled task switches on its own, so pick Sonnet 5.5 in the ones you still use before then.

Disclosure: ConvoShelf is our product. The Claude details come from Anthropic's help center article Claude Sonnet 4.6 is leaving the model selector (updated November 25, 2026) and the other Anthropic pages listed under Sources, all read on November 26, 2026. The ConvoShelf details come from its Chrome Web Store listing for version 1.2.0.

What changes on December 8

  • Until December 8 you can still choose Sonnet 4.6 in the model selector.
  • From December 8 it no longer appears there. This covers chat and Claude Cowork on the Free, Pro, Max and Ultra plans, in the browser, Claude Desktop and Claude Mobile.
  • Not affected: Claude Code, Team and Enterprise plans, and the Claude Platform API keep Sonnet 4.6.

Anthropic did the same with Haiku 4.5 on October 29, 2026: it left the selector, Haiku 5.5 replaced it, and old Haiku 4.5 chats continued on Haiku 5.5 with nothing deleted, according to the Haiku 4.5 help article.

Switch a chat to Sonnet 5.5 before December 8

  1. Open the chat on claude.ai, in Claude Desktop or in the mobile app.
  2. Click the model name next to the send button.
  3. Choose Claude Sonnet 5.5, or click More models if you don't see it.
  4. Send your next message. Anthropic says a model change applies starting with Claude's next response.

Do the same in any scheduled tasks that run on Sonnet 4.6. If you have dozens of old chats, you don't need to touch the ones you're finished with: they stay readable in your history either way.

What's different about Sonnet 5.5

Anthropic released Sonnet 5.5 on September 28, 2026. Its announcement calls it a clear upgrade over Sonnet 5 that generates output more than 30% faster, and says Medium effort is the default in the Claude apps.

One behavior to know before you move a sensitive chat: Sonnet 5.5 can switch a flagged request to Sonnet 5. Anthropic's model switching article says you see a notice, the reply is labeled with the model that answered, and the chat stays on Sonnet 5 until you switch back. You can turn this off in Settings > Capabilities with the Switch models when a message is flagged toggle; the chat then pauses instead.

Keep a copy of the chats that matter

Nothing is being deleted, so a backup is optional. If you want one anyway, Anthropic's export steps are:

  1. On the web app or Claude Desktop, go to Settings > Privacy. The iOS and Android apps can't run an export.
  2. Click Export data.
  3. Download the file from the email link right away. Anthropic says the link expires 24 hours after it's created and may work only once.

The export includes your conversations but not the original files you uploaded. For more on this, see how to back up Claude chats.

Where ConvoShelf fits

ConvoShelf is a browser extension that adds folders, tags and search to claude.ai, chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com and grok.com. It can't keep Sonnet 4.6 in your model list and it doesn't change your Claude account. It helps with the sorting:

  • As you open each old chat to switch its model, press + on a folder to file it, or tag it, so you know which ones you've already moved.
  • Search covers the words inside chats you've opened since installing, plus chat titles from Claude's sidebar.
  • The free plan exports the open chat to Markdown or PDF (the messages loaded on screen). Pro, $4.99 once, exports a whole folder at once as Markdown or JSON.

Folders, tags and saved chat text stay in your browser; there is no ConvoShelf account or server. ConvoShelf can't import Claude's official data export. For the full setup, see how to organize Claude chats into folders.

FAQ

Will my Claude Sonnet 4.6 chats be deleted on December 8?

No. Anthropic's help center says your Sonnet 4.6 chats stay in your account and nothing is deleted. From December 8 they move to Sonnet 5.5 or your default model as you continue them.

Can I still use Sonnet 4.6 after December 8?

Not from the model selector on the Free, Pro, Max or Ultra plans. Anthropic says Sonnet 4.6 stays available in Claude Code, on Team and Enterprise plans, and on the Claude Platform API.

What replaces Sonnet 4.6?

Anthropic recommends Claude Sonnet 5.5, released September 28, 2026. It describes Sonnet 5.5 as a clear upgrade over Sonnet 5 that generates output more than 30% faster.

Do I have to switch each chat by hand?

Anthropic says not every chat, task or scheduled task will switch automatically, and recommends choosing Sonnet 5.5 in each one before December 8. Open the chat, click the model name next to the send button and pick Sonnet 5.5.

Can ConvoShelf keep Sonnet 4.6 for me?

No. ConvoShelf is a folders and search extension. It can't change which models Claude offers. It can file and search the Claude chats you open, and export the open chat to Markdown or PDF.
