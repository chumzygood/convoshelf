GPT-5.6 Sol retires Dec 23: what happens to your ChatGPT chats

Published November 24, 2026 · 4-minute read

On December 23, 2026, OpenAI is removing GPT-5.6 Sol from Chat in ChatGPT, and GPT-5.6 Sol and Terra from Work and Codex, but nothing in the announcement deletes your chats: they stay in your account and, per OpenAI, can carry over to newer models. If you want your own copy of those conversations, request a ChatGPT data export before the date.

Disclosure: ConvoShelf is our product. The retirement date comes from the @ChatGPT post on X of November 23, 2026, which we read on November 24. The ChatGPT export steps come from OpenAI's Help Center article Exporting your ChatGPT history and data.

What OpenAI announced

On November 23, 2026, the official ChatGPT account posted:

"Every model has its day in the sun. For GPT-5.6, it's time to set. On December 23, we're deprecating GPT-5.6 Sol in Chat, and GPT-5.6 Sol and Terra in Work and Codex."

In a follow-up reply, the account explained the policy: "As we roll out new models, we'll generally continue to retire models that are two generations back. That frees up compute so more people can use our more capable models."

The same day, 9to5Mac reported that GPT-6.2 Sol was coming to the Chat tab, with the rollout starting for ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise users.

What changes, and for whom

  • Chat. GPT-5.6 Sol leaves Chat on December 23. If you pick it by name from the model menu, you'll need to pick another model after that.
  • Work and Codex. Both GPT-5.6 Sol and GPT-5.6 Terra leave on the same day. Anything set to use those two models by name will need a new choice.
  • Not covered by the post. It gives no time of day and doesn't mention GPT-5.6 Luna or the OpenAI API.

TechRadar reported that some fans are mourning the model. The announcement doesn't mention any change of plan, so plan around December 23.

Your chats stay; the model that answers changes

Your conversations live in your ChatGPT account, not inside a model. OpenAI's help page What to expect when models change says that "your prior conversations can carry over to newer models, including any background you’ve shared about your interests, preferences, or goals." It also warns that "tone or style may change".

So a long chat you started with GPT-5.6 Sol should still be in your sidebar after December 23. If you continue it, a newer model writes the next reply, and it may not sound the same. Nothing in the announcement says chats get deleted.

Keep your own copy before December 23

You don't have to do anything to keep your chats in ChatGPT. A data export is still worth having if those conversations matter to you, because it gives you a copy outside your account. OpenAI's export steps:

  1. Open your profile menu in ChatGPT and select Settings.
  2. Select Data controls.
  3. Under Export data, select Export, then Confirm export.

OpenAI says the export can take up to 7 days to arrive, and the download link expires 24 hours after you receive it, so download it when it lands. The ZIP includes an index.html viewer you can open in your browser to search your conversations. If the ZIP won't open, see ChatGPT data export ZIP incomplete or won't extract.

An export copies the text of your conversations. It can't keep a model's way of answering, so treat it as a record, not a way to keep talking to GPT-5.6 Sol.

Where ConvoShelf fits

ConvoShelf is a browser extension for chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com and grok.com. We checked what it does with ChatGPT chats in the 1.2.0 code, the version on the Chrome Web Store:

  • It saves chats you open. When you open a ChatGPT chat at chatgpt.com, ConvoShelf keeps a copy of its text in your browser on this computer. There is no ConvoShelf account or server.

FAQ

When is GPT-5.6 Sol being retired?

December 23, 2026. The @ChatGPT account said on November 23 that it is deprecating GPT-5.6 Sol in Chat, and GPT-5.6 Sol and Terra in Work and Codex, that day. It did not give a time of day.

Will I lose my chats with GPT-5.6 Sol?

Nothing in the announcement says chats are deleted. Your chats are stored in your ChatGPT account, and OpenAI's help page says prior conversations can carry over to newer models. What changes is the model that answers.

Can I keep using GPT-5.6 Sol after December 23?

Not in Chat, Work or Codex, according to the announcement. The post did not mention the OpenAI API, so it says nothing either way about developer access.

How do I back up my ChatGPT chats before the retirement?

In ChatGPT, open Settings, then Data controls, and under Export data select Export. OpenAI says the export can take up to 7 days to arrive, and the download link expires 24 hours after you receive it.

Does ConvoShelf keep my GPT-5.6 chats?

ConvoShelf keeps its own copy of chats you open at chatgpt.com in Chrome, and it can import the ZIP from a ChatGPT data export, so those chats stay searchable on your computer. It can't bring back a model or change your ChatGPT account.
