Privacy policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

ConvoShelf is a Chrome extension that organizes, searches and exports your AI chat conversations.

What it reads

On chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com and grok.com, ConvoShelf reads the chat titles in the sidebar and the messages of the chat you have open, so it can file, search and export them.

Where it is stored

Folders, tags, pins, saved prompts and chat text are stored in chrome.storage.local on your computer. Nothing is uploaded. ConvoShelf has no server and no account.

Network requests

One only: when you press Confirm on a Pro license key, ConvoShelf sends that key and our Polar organization ID to Polar (api.polar.sh) to check it, and re-checks it every 3 days. Chrome asks your permission first. Payment is handled by Polar under its own privacy policy.

What we do not do

The extension has no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no selling or transferring data and no remote code, and it still sends nothing to us. The website (not the extension) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It sets no cookies and keeps no personal data.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all ConvoShelf data, or remove the extension.

X Bookmarks Shelf, Saved Shelf for Reddit and Watch Later Shelf

The same rules apply to each Shelf extension. They read only the list page you open (your X bookmarks, your Reddit saved page, a YouTube playlist) and store what they read in your browser's local storage. They have no account, no server and no analytics. Saved Shelf's import asks reddit.com for your own saved list while you are signed in; that request goes to Reddit, never to us. The only other requests any of them make go to Polar (api.polar.sh), and only if you use Pro: one when you confirm a Pro license key, then one every 3 days to re-check that key. If Polar reports the key as not found, the extension asks once more 12 hours later before it turns Pro off. Each request carries only the license key and our Polar organization ID. Chrome asks your permission before the first one.
