Switching ChatGPT accounts? How to move or back up chats between them

Updated October 31, 2026 · 4-minute read

ChatGPT now lets you stay signed in to two accounts and switch between them, but switching never moves a chat from one account to the other. If you want old chats available in the account you're switching to, the only route OpenAI offers is to export them and upload the file as reference in a new chat.

Disclosure: ConvoShelf is our product. Everything about ChatGPT here comes from OpenAI's help articles on using multiple ChatGPT accounts and transferring exported conversations, which we read on October 31, 2026.

How account switching works

OpenAI says you can stay signed in to up to 2 ChatGPT accounts at a time and switch without signing out. Only one is active at a time. It works on ChatGPT on the web and is rolling out in the iOS and Android apps, so if you don't see Switch account in the app's Settings yet, you may not have it.

  • Web: open the account menu, select Add account, and sign in or create the second account. To switch, open the account menu and pick the other account.
  • iOS or Android: open Settings, select Switch account, then Add account. To switch later, go back to Switch account and pick the other one.

A new account starts on the Free plan. If two accounts are already signed in, you have to log out of one before adding a third. On the web, an administrator can turn switching off for a managed work workspace.

What stays separate

When you switch, chats, memory, files and settings all change to the selected account. OpenAI lists what switching does not do:

  • Move chats between accounts
  • Share memory or conversation history
  • Share files or uploads
  • Combine billing or subscriptions
  • Merge accounts or workspaces

OpenAI also suggests checking which account is active before you start a conversation or buy anything. If the work account is managed by your company, the chats you start there follow its policies, and switching doesn't change that.

Moving chats to the other account: export, then upload

OpenAI says ChatGPT doesn't support merging accounts or moving conversations from one account's history into another's. What it offers instead:

  1. Export from the first account. In Settings, open Data controls, select Export and confirm the export. OpenAI says the export can take up to 7 days to arrive, and the download link expires 24 hours after you get it. Download it while signed in to the same account.
  2. Find the conversations file. Extract the ZIP and find conversations.json. Larger exports may have numbered conversation JSON files instead.
  3. Upload it in the second account. Switch to the personal account you want to keep, start a new chat and upload the file. ChatGPT can use it as reference in that one chat.

The old chats don't come back as separate chats, the sidebar isn't restored, and subscriptions, memories, custom instructions and GPTs don't move. If the file is too big to upload, OpenAI suggests uploading the numbered files separately, splitting the file, or uploading only the conversations you need. Exports aren't available from ChatGPT Business or Enterprise workspaces, so you can't do this with chats in a company workspace.

FAQ

How many ChatGPT accounts can I be signed in to at once?

Two. OpenAI says you can stay signed in to up to 2 ChatGPT accounts and switch between them, with only one active at a time. You can own more accounts, but you have to log out of one before adding a third.

Does switching ChatGPT accounts move my chats?

No. OpenAI says switching does not move chats between accounts, share memory or conversation history, share files, combine billing, or merge accounts. Each account keeps its own chats.

Can I move chats from one ChatGPT account to another?

Not into the other account's chat history. OpenAI's route is to export your data from the first account, then upload conversations.json to a new chat in the second personal account, where ChatGPT can use it as reference. It does not recreate the old chats in the sidebar.

Why don't I see Switch account in the ChatGPT app?

OpenAI says account switching is still rolling out on iOS and Android, so it may not be available to you yet. On the web, a workspace administrator can also turn it off for a managed workspace.

Does a second ChatGPT account cost money?

Creating one doesn't. OpenAI says a new account starts on the Free plan. A paid plan stays with the account that bought it, so a second paid account needs its own subscription.
