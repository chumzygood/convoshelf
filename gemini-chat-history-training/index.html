Gemini chat history: the new History and training settings explained

Updated November 18, 2026 · 6-minute read

Google is replacing Gemini's single Keep Activity setting with a History setting and a separate AI-training setting, so you can keep your chat history without it being used to train Google's models, and with History off your future chats are now kept for around two months instead of 72 hours.

Google announced the change on November 16, 2026 and says it will roll out globally in the Gemini app "in the coming weeks", so your account may still show the older Keep Activity setting for a while.

Disclosure: ConvoShelf is our product. The Gemini facts here come from Google's announcement, the Gemini Apps Privacy Hub (last updated November 17, 2026) and the Gemini Help pages listed under Sources, all read on November 18, 2026. This page first went up on November 4, when the Privacy Hub still gave 72 hours.

What changed on November 16

Until now, one setting, Keep Activity, decided both whether Gemini saved your chats and whether Google could use them to train its AI models. 9to5Google and TechCrunch both describe it that way. Google is splitting it in two:

  • History saves your chats so you can scroll back through them, and sets how long they're kept. Google's history help page says it is on by default if you're 18 or over.
  • Chat content including text & uploads is the training setting. The same help page says it is off by default and can only be turned on while History is on. A second box covers audio and Gemini Live videos and screenshares.

The other half of the update is retention. With History off, Google now keeps future chats with your account for around two months. TechCrunch compared that with the 72 hours in an archived copy of the Privacy Hub from November 12. We saw the same 72-hour figure when we wrote this page on November 4.

What happens to your current choice: TechCrunch reports Google saying both new settings will be off for people who had Keep Activity off. If you had Keep Activity on, your history stays on and you decide about training separately.

Is my Gemini chat history used for training?

Only if the training setting is on. When it is, the Privacy Hub says Google uses the content of your future chats to train generative AI models: your prompts, what you attach, Gemini's responses, tasks and tool calls, location info and data from connected apps. Chats are separated from your account before they go to service providers, and reviewed chats are kept for up to 3 years, even if you delete them or turn the setting off later.

With the setting off, Google says your future chats won't be used to train generative AI models unless you send feedback. They are still reviewed and used to improve Google services in other ways and to protect Google, its users and the public.

Check your settings on a computer

Export first if you want a copy (see below), because one of the options deletes your history.

  1. Go to gemini.google.com and click Settings, then History. You can also go straight to myactivity.google.com/product/gemini.
  2. To keep history but stay out of training, leave History on and make sure the box next to Chat content including text & uploads is not selected. Google says you can turn these training settings off at any time.
  3. To stop saving history, click On near the top, then Turn off, or Turn off and delete history to also remove what's saved.
  4. While you're there, check auto-delete. Google deletes history older than 18 months by default; you can pick 3 or 36 months or choose not to auto-delete.

If you only see Keep Activity (or Gemini Apps Activity), the update hasn't reached your account yet. That older setting still controls history and training together, so turning it off is the way to keep new chats out of training until the new settings arrive.

With History off, Google's help page lists the connected apps that still work: device assistance, Gemini Notebook, Messages, Phone and WhatsApp. Others stop working until you turn History back on.

Temporary chats

A temporary chat keeps one conversation out of your history while the rest stay saved. On gemini.google.com, click Temporary chat next to New chat. According to Google:

  • temporary chats aren't used to train its AI models, and they're now kept with your account for around two months, the same as chats with History off;
  • they don't show in your recent chats, and you can't get back to one once you leave it;
  • you can't use Gems, connected apps or personalization in one, and you can't give feedback.

Temporary chats need a personal Google Account. Google says they aren't available on work or school accounts, in Gemini in Chrome or in Gemini in Google Messages.

What these settings don't stop

Whatever you choose, the Privacy Hub says Google uses your chats to respond to you and to help protect Google, its users and the public, including with help from human reviewers. With History off, Gemini uses the last 24 hours of a chat as context. Google also gives a system failure as one reason it keeps chats for about two months.

  • Feedback. If you send feedback, Google collects it with context that can include the last 24 hours of your chats, has trained teams review it, and keeps it for up to 3 years.
  • Reviewed chats. Chats a reviewer looked at are disconnected from your account, kept for up to 3 years, and not deleted when you delete your history.
  • Anonymized data. Google says your Gemini settings don't control processing of your chats to create anonymized data.
  • Other Google settings. Changing Gemini's settings doesn't change Web & App Activity, Search Services History or Timeline, which may keep saving data from other Google services.
  • Not for ads. Google says your Gemini chats aren't used to show you ads, and that it will tell you if that changes.

Does a paid plan change anything?

Not according to Google's notice. The Gemini Apps Privacy Hub covers signed-in personal Google Accounts, and it lists "subscription information" for people who pay for a Google AI plan as one of the things Google collects. It doesn't describe a separate training rule for paying subscribers; the controls are the same History, training and temporary chat settings.

Work and school accounts are a different case. Google says they may be subject to different data handling terms, and on those accounts the Google Workspace administrator controls the History settings.

Export your Gemini history, then delete it

Download a copy with Google Takeout before you delete anything:

  1. Go to takeout.google.com and click Deselect all.
  2. Check My Activity, click All activity data included, click Deselect all, check Gemini Apps, then OK. To include your Gems, also check Gemini.
  3. Click Next step, choose email, Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or Box, then Create export.

Google says an export can take a few hours to a few days, though most people get the link the same day, and an emailed link stays active for 7 days. Downloading doesn't delete anything.

To delete, go to Settings > History on gemini.google.com, or to myactivity.google.com/product/gemini. You can delete one item, one day, the last hour or day, a custom range, or all time. Deleting a shared chat doesn't delete its public link, so remove shared links separately. For the same cleanup on ChatGPT, see whether deleting a ChatGPT chat deletes its memories.

Where ConvoShelf fits

ConvoShelf doesn't change any Gemini setting and can't stop Google from keeping or using your chats. It's a browser extension that keeps its own copy of chats you open, in your browser on this computer. We checked the code of version 1.2.0, the version the Chrome Web Store lists, on November 18, 2026.

  • When you open a saved Gemini chat at gemini.google.com in desktop Chrome, ConvoShelf saves its text in your browser's local storage, next to your ChatGPT, Claude and Grok chats, so you can search and reread it later.
  • That copy has no account and no ConvoShelf server behind it. Deleting your Gemini history doesn't remove it, and Google's History setting doesn't change it. Version 1.2.0 has no button to remove one saved chat; its settings page has Delete all ConvoShelf data.
  • The free plan exports the open chat as Markdown or PDF. Pro, $4.99 once, adds JSON export and exports of a folder or every saved chat.
  • It only has chats you've opened, it doesn't work in the Gemini phone app, and it doesn't replace Google Takeout.

FAQ

Is my Gemini chat history used to train AI?

Only if the training setting, Chat content including text & uploads, is on. Google's help page says it is off by default and needs History on. With it off, Google says future chats aren't used to train generative AI models unless you send feedback.

How long does Google keep Gemini chats with History off?

Around two months. Google announced the change on November 16, 2026 and is rolling it out globally over the coming weeks. Before that, the Privacy Hub said 72 hours.

Can I keep my Gemini chat history but opt out of training?

Yes, once the update reaches your account. Leave History on in Settings > History and leave the Chat content including text & uploads box unselected. Until then, the older Keep Activity setting controls both together.

Are Gemini temporary chats used for training?

No. Google says temporary chats aren't used to train its AI models. They're now kept with your account for around two months, don't show in your recent chats, and you lose access to one once you leave it.

How do I download my Gemini chat history?

Use Google Takeout. Under My Activity, open All activity data included, deselect everything, select Gemini Apps, then create the export. Google says most people get the link the same day, and downloading doesn't delete anything.
