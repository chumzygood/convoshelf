How to save part of a ChatGPT answer and find it again

Published November 26, 2026 · 4-minute read

To save part of a ChatGPT answer, select the lines you want and keep them somewhere that also remembers which chat they came from: ChatGPT has no built-in way to save a highlighted passage, so either paste it into a note with the chat's link, or use an extension like ConvoShelf that saves the selection as a clip with a Jump back link.

Disclosure: ConvoShelf is our product. The ChatGPT sharing details come from OpenAI's help article Sharing conversations and scheduled tasks in ChatGPT, read November 26, 2026. The ConvoShelf steps were checked against the 1.2.0 extension code the same day.

Why copy and paste isn't enough

Copying a good paragraph into a notes app works until you need it again. The note has no link back to the chat, so you can't see the question that produced it or the follow-ups that changed it, and weeks later you don't remember which of fifty conversations it came from. If you use more than one AI, the hunt covers ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.

What ChatGPT gives you

  • Copy. Every answer has a copy button. You get the text, nothing else.
  • Share one response. OpenAI says a shared link can be limited to an individual assistant response instead of the full conversation, when that option is available. On a personal account, anyone with the link can view it, and the link has no expiry date, so it's for sending to someone, not for private notes.
  • Keep the whole chat. Leave it in your history or move it into a project, and come back to it. That keeps everything, not the three lines you needed.

None of these saves a highlighted passage on its own with a way back to where it was.

Save the useful part as a clip

ConvoShelf is a browser extension that adds a panel to chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com and grok.com. Its Clips tab keeps a selected passage together with the chat it came from:

  1. Open the chat and select the lines you want inside one AI answer.
  2. Press Alt+Shift+S (or click the ConvoShelf icon) to open the panel, then open the Clips tab.
  3. Click Save selected answer text. The clip's label starts as the first 50 characters of the text; click Label to rename it.
  4. Later, type a word in Search clips. It matches the label and the clip text.
  5. Click Jump back to open the chat the clip came from and go to that answer.

A few limits from the code: the selection has to sit inside a single AI answer (your own message, or text spanning two answers, won't save), and one clip holds up to 12,000 characters.

The free plan keeps 10 clips. Pro, $4.99 once, keeps unlimited clips and adds Export clips, which saves every clip to one Markdown file with its label, the text and the source chat's address. Clips also feed ConvoShelf's Handoff: when you continue a chat in another AI, the last 10 clips from that chat go into the summary it builds.

What it can't do: clips stay in this browser on this computer. There is no ConvoShelf account or server, so they don't sync to your phone. If you delete the original chat in ChatGPT, Jump back has nothing to open, though the clip text stays.

Which one to use

  • Sending an answer to someone: share the single response from ChatGPT.
  • A recipe, a command, a paragraph you'll reuse: save it as a clip, so it's searchable and one click from its chat.
  • A prompt you'll run again: that's a saved prompt, not a clip. See how to keep a ChatGPT prompt library.
  • The whole conversation: file it in a folder or export it. See how to export a ChatGPT conversation.

FAQ

Can I save just part of a ChatGPT answer?

ChatGPT itself lets you copy a response or share a link to it, but it has no built-in way to save a highlighted part of an answer. A browser extension such as ConvoShelf can save the selected lines as a clip with a link back to the chat.

Can I share only one ChatGPT response instead of the whole chat?

Sometimes. OpenAI's help center says that if you share an individual assistant response, the link can be limited to that response rather than the full conversation, when that sharing option is available. Anyone with the link can view it.

How many clips can I save for free in ConvoShelf?

The free plan keeps 10 clips, with search and Jump back included. Pro, $4.99 once, keeps unlimited clips and adds export of all clips to one Markdown file.

Does a clip work on Claude, Gemini and Grok too?

Yes. ConvoShelf clips work on chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com and grok.com. The selection has to sit inside one AI answer; text from your own message or from two answers at once won't save.

Where are clips stored?

In your browser's extension storage on this computer. ConvoShelf has no account and no server, so clips don't sync to your phone or another computer. Back them up with the Pro Markdown export or the options page backup.
