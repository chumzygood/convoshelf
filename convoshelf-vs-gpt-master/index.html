ConvoShelf vs GPT Master

GPT Master is a toolkit for ChatGPT only: folders and subfolders, plus Side Chat, a minimap, bulk delete and archive, and a prompt optimizer, and it creates an account for you when you install it. ConvoShelf files chats from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok in one set of folders, searches the words inside them, and keeps everything in your browser with no account. Pro is $4.99 once. If you only use ChatGPT and want more tools inside each chat, GPT Master covers more. If you use more than one AI, or want no account at all, ConvoShelf fits better.

Where GPT Master does more

GPT Master adds tools inside the ChatGPT page that ConvoShelf doesn't have. Side Chat lets you ask about one paragraph of a reply without adding to the main chat. The minimap lists your prompts so you can jump around a long thread, and timestamps show when each message was sent. It can also delete or archive many ChatGPT chats at once, which ConvoShelf can't.

Where ConvoShelf does more

ConvoShelf works on Claude, Gemini and Grok as well as ChatGPT, and one folder can hold chats from all four. It runs on Firefox too. There is no account and no ConvoShelf server: folders, tags and saved chat text stay in your browser, and the only network request is the Pro license check. You can also import the ZIP that ChatGPT emails you and search years of old chats on your computer.

How to pick

  • Only ChatGPT, and you want Side Chat, a minimap or bulk delete: GPT Master.
  • Two or more of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok: ConvoShelf.
  • You'd rather have no account, or you use Firefox: ConvoShelf.
  • You want to know the price before you install: ConvoShelf lists $4.99 once. GPT Master's site says some features are paid but doesn't show a price.

FAQ

Is ConvoShelf a GPT Master alternative?

Yes, for folders and search. ConvoShelf files and searches chats on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok. It doesn't have GPT Master's Side Chat, minimap or bulk delete.

Does GPT Master work on Claude or Gemini?

Its site and Chrome Web Store listing describe it as working on chatgpt.com. We found no mention of Claude, Gemini or Grok.

Do I need an account?

Not for ConvoShelf. GPT Master has no sign-up step, but its FAQ says it creates an account for you on install, and Google sign-in is optional.

How much does each cost?

ConvoShelf is free, and Pro is $4.99 once with no subscription. GPT Master says its core features are free and some features are paid; its site doesn't list a price.

Can I use both?

Yes. Both run on chatgpt.com, and GPT Master's FAQ says other ChatGPT extensions can work alongside it, though two extensions changing the same part of the page can clash.
