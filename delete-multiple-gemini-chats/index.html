How to delete multiple Gemini chats at once

Published November 28, 2026 · 4-minute read · Leia em português

As of November 28, 2026, Gemini has no way to tick several chats and delete them together, on gemini.google.com or in the app. Google's help pages give two ways to remove more than one chat: delete a block of Gemini History by time (the last hour, the last day, one day, a custom range or all time), or delete chats one at a time from the side panel. A multi-select option has shown up in a test build of the Google app for Android, but Google hasn't released it.

Disclosure: ConvoShelf is our product. The Gemini steps come from Google's Gemini Apps Help articles Manage & delete your history in Gemini Apps and Find & manage your recent chats in Gemini Apps, both read on November 28, 2026. The ConvoShelf details come from its Chrome Web Store listing for version 1.4.1 (updated November 28, 2026).

Delete many chats by date in Gemini History

This is the closest thing Gemini has to bulk delete. On a computer:

  1. Go to gemini.google.com.
  2. At the bottom of the side panel, click Settings, then History. You can also go straight to myactivity.google.com/product/gemini.
  3. Above your history, click Delete and pick Last hour, Last day, Custom range or All time.
  4. To clear one day, click Delete all history from next to that day. To remove a single entry, click Delete history item next to it.

A range takes everything inside it. If three of the forty chats from last March are ones you still need, a custom range for March deletes those three as well, pinned or not. Google says that once data in Gemini History is deleted, the chats no longer appear in your recent and pinned chats.

Delete chats one at a time

For a handful of chats spread across different dates, this is quicker than picking ranges.

  • On gemini.google.com: open the side panel (click Menu if it's hidden), hover over the chat, click More, then Delete.
  • In the Gemini app: tap Menu, touch and hold the chat under "Recent", then tap Delete.

Deleting a chat from the side panel also deletes it from Gemini History.

What a deleted chat takes with it

  • Anything you created in that chat, such as Canvas docs and apps, goes too.
  • Branched chats are separate. Deleting the original doesn't delete a branch, and deleting a branch doesn't delete the original.
  • Deleting a shared chat doesn't delete its public link. Remove shared links separately.
  • Deleting a Gemini Live chat also deletes its audio, transcript, video and screen shares.

Some data outlasts the delete button. Chats a human reviewer looked at are kept separately for up to 3 years. Our guide to Gemini's History and training settings covers what Google keeps and why.

On a work or school Google Account, your Google Workspace administrator controls Gemini History, and Google says you can't delete chats yourself.

Let auto-delete clear old chats for you

By default Google auto-deletes Gemini history older than 18 months. In Settings > History, click the auto-delete option near the top and choose 3, 18 or 36 months, or turn auto-delete off. A 3-month setting keeps the chat list short without any manual cleanup, but it also removes old chats you might want later, so export those first.

The Select option Google is testing

On October 26, 2026, Android Authority reported code for a Select button in version 17.70.10 of the Google app for Android. It would let you tick several chats in the Gemini chat list and delete them together. The report says it isn't clear whether the web version will get it, and features found this way sometimes never ship. Google hasn't announced it, and the help pages we read on November 28 don't mention it.

Save the chats you want before a big delete

Google Takeout can download your Gemini history; the steps are in our Gemini history settings guide. Takeout gives you one large archive. If you only want a few chats as readable files, exporting them one by one is often easier.

FAQ

Can I select several Gemini chats and delete them together?

Not yet. Google's Gemini help pages describe deleting one chat at a time from the side panel, or deleting Gemini History by time. A Select option was found in a test build of the Google app for Android in October 2026, but Google hasn't released it.

How do I delete all my Gemini chats at once?

On gemini.google.com, click Settings, then History (or go to myactivity.google.com/product/gemini). Above your history, click Delete and choose All time.

Does deleting Gemini history also remove pinned chats?

Yes, if they fall in the range you delete. Google says that once data in Gemini History is deleted, those chats no longer appear in your recent and pinned chats. Pinning doesn't protect a chat.

Why can't I delete chats on my work Gemini account?

On a work or school Google Account, Gemini History is controlled by your Google Workspace administrator, and Google says you can't delete chats yourself.

Can ConvoShelf delete Gemini chats for me?

No. ConvoShelf adds folders, tags, search and export to Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude and Grok. It doesn't delete Gemini chats. Use it to export the chats you want to keep before you delete.
