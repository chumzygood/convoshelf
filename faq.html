FAQ

How do I install it?

Get it from the Chrome Web Store: Add to Chrome, free. Version 1.2.0 is live on Chrome, Firefox and Edge. The free plan has no time limit. Pro is $4.99, paid once.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no ConvoShelf account and no ConvoShelf server.

Where are my folders stored?

In your browser's local extension storage. Use Backup in settings to save a JSON file; Restore merges it back.

Does it work with ChatGPT Projects?

Yes. Chats inside a Project can be filed, searched and exported like any other chat.

Why doesn't search find an old chat?

For ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok, ConvoShelf indexes a chat's text when you open it. Open older chats once and they become searchable. Titles from the sidebar are picked up as you browse. To search older ChatGPT chats without opening each one, ask ChatGPT for your data export (Settings, Data controls, Export data), then choose the ZIP in ConvoShelf's settings. ConvoShelf reads the file on your computer. Nothing is uploaded. Importing is free, with a cap of 20,000 imported chats; a very long imported chat keeps its most recent 400,000 characters. Search of chats you have opened is capped at 3,000 chats. Official exports from Claude, Gemini or Grok cannot be imported.

How do I import my ChatGPT export?

In ChatGPT, open Settings, then Data controls, and choose Export data. When the ZIP arrives, open ConvoShelf's options (from the toolbar icon) and choose that ZIP under Import. You can cancel mid-import; a cancelled or damaged file leaves your library as it was.

What is the command palette?

Press Alt+Shift+K (if your browser set that key; you can change it on your browser's shortcuts page) or the ⌨ button in the panel to jump to a chat, run a prompt or chain, file the open chat, export it, or start a search. ConvoShelf never presses Send for you.

How do I export a long chat?

Scroll to the top of the chat so every message is loaded, then use Export. PDF opens the print dialog; pick "Save as PDF".

Which sites?

chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com and grok.com.

What does the free plan include?

Unlimited top-level folders (creating new subfolders is Pro; any you already have stay usable), full-text search of chats you have opened since installing (up to 3,000 chats; titles sooner) plus any ChatGPT export you import (up to 20,000 imported chats; a very long imported chat keeps its most recent 400,000 characters), export of the open chat to Markdown or PDF, 10 saved prompts (a prompt chain counts as one), 10 clips and 1 handoff a day, with no time limit. The free plan also includes a command palette and blanks in prompts. ConvoShelf never presses Send.

What does Pro cost?

$4.99, paid once. It adds subfolders (creating new ones; any you already have stay usable), bulk export, JSON export, search filters for opened and imported chats, jump to the exact message from a search result, bulk actions, folder colors, unlimited prompts and chains, unlimited clips, clip export, and one-click handoff with unlimited copies. Importing a ChatGPT export, the command palette and prompt chains are on the free plan.
