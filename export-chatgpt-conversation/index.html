How to export a ChatGPT conversation to a file

To export your ChatGPT conversations, sign in, open Settings, then Data controls, and choose Export data. OpenAI emails or texts a download link when the zip is ready, which can take up to 7 days, and the link expires 24 hours after it arrives. Those steps come from OpenAI's Help Center article Exporting your ChatGPT history and data, re-read on 13 November 2026. The page said it had been updated 12 days earlier.

If you only need a copy of the chat you have open, you can also save that one file right away. We make a Chrome extension, ConvoShelf, that exports the open chat as Markdown or PDF, for free. That is a copy of chats you open, not a replacement for OpenAI's full account export.

How to request OpenAI's official export

  1. Sign in to ChatGPT in a browser, on the same account whose data you want.
  2. Open Settings, then Data controls.
  3. Choose Export data, then confirm.
  4. Wait for the email or text with the download link. OpenAI says this can take up to 7 days.
  5. Open the link and download the zip while you are still signed into that same account. The link expires 24 hours after it arrives.

OpenAI's article, as of today, says this export is available to ChatGPT Free, Go, Plus, and Pro, and to eligible ChatGPT Edu accounts. It is not available for ChatGPT Business, Enterprise, or Healthcare. Those plans are directed to OpenAI's Privacy Portal, which is linked from the same Help Center page.

What is in the zip, and what it will not do

The Help Center says the zip includes your chat history and other relevant account data. A related OpenAI article, How to transfer ChatGPT chats from one account to another on ChatGPT Edu, names conversations.json as the file that holds conversations. We are describing what those two articles say, not unpacking a sample zip of our own in this post.

You cannot use the export to restore chats you already deleted. The Help Center is clear on that. If you want a lasting copy of a conversation, export it before you delete it.

The official zip is also not a one-click move into Claude, Gemini, or Grok. It is a copy of your ChatGPT account data, delivered by OpenAI.

Saving one open chat as Markdown or PDF, checked today

If you want a file of the chat on your screen, without waiting for the zip, ConvoShelf can do that on the computer where the chat is open. This is what version 1.1.0 does, checked against our live site and Pro page on 13 November 2026:

  • Open the chat, and scroll to the top so every message has loaded.
  • Press Export in the ConvoShelf panel, and pick Markdown or PDF. PDF opens Chrome's print dialog; choose Save as PDF.
  • The free plan covers that single-chat export. Pro is $4.99, paid once, with no subscription, and adds JSON plus bulk export of a folder or every chat.
  • There is no ConvoShelf account. The chats stay in your browser. The same extension also works on Claude, Gemini, and Grok. We have a separate guide on backing up Claude chats and one on organizing ChatGPT chats into folders.

Use the official OpenAI export when you want a complete copy of your ChatGPT history. Use the local export when you want a file of one chat now. They solve different jobs. If your ChatGPT plan is changing, we also wrote about exporting chats around a plan change.
