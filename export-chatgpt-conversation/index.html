How to export a ChatGPT conversation and keep chats from getting lost

To keep ChatGPT chats from getting lost, request OpenAI's export (Settings, Data controls, Export data), check that the ZIP holds the chats you need, and save a file of any chat that matters, because a chat you delete can't be restored.

OpenAI emails or texts a download link when the ZIP is ready. That can take up to 7 days, and the link expires 24 hours after it arrives. Those steps come from OpenAI's Help Center article Exporting your ChatGPT history and data, re-read on 21 November 2026, when it said it had been updated 4 days earlier.

If you only need a copy of the chat you have open, you can save that one file right away. We make a Chrome extension, ConvoShelf, that exports the open chat as Markdown or PDF, for free. Once you have OpenAI's ZIP, you can also import it into ConvoShelf and search those chats on your computer. The ZIP remains OpenAI's official copy of your account; ConvoShelf's import does not put chats back into ChatGPT, and it cannot restore chats you already deleted.

How to request OpenAI's official export

  1. Sign in to ChatGPT on the web, on the same account whose data you want. The export isn't available while you're logged out.
  2. Open your profile menu, then Settings, then Data controls.
  3. Under Export data, select Export, then Confirm export.
  4. Wait for the email or text with the download link. OpenAI says this can take up to 7 days. Check spam and promotions too.
  5. Select Download data export while you are still signed in to that same account. The link expires 24 hours after you receive it.

The ChatGPT apps on iPhone and Android have the same Export data option under Data controls. If the app offers Open ChatGPT on web instead, finish the steps on the web.

OpenAI's article says this export is available for ChatGPT Free, Go, Plus and Pro, and eligible ChatGPT Edu workspaces. Self-service export is not available in ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or ChatGPT for Healthcare workspaces; OpenAI tells those users to contact their workspace owner. Consumer accounts also have a second route, the Privacy Portal (Make a Privacy Request, then I have a consumer ChatGPT account, then Download my data), which OpenAI points to if you can't sign in.

What is in the ZIP, and what it will not do

OpenAI says the ZIP includes your chat history and other relevant account data. As of this update, it also contains an export viewer, index.html: extract the ZIP, open that file in your browser, select Choose folder and pick the extracted folder, and you can search your conversations there. OpenAI says the viewer works locally and doesn't upload anything. The conversations themselves are in JSON format. Earlier exports may contain chat.html instead.

You cannot use the export to restore chats you already deleted. OpenAI says so twice on the same page, and asks you to check the export's contents before deleting chats or moving to a managed workspace.

The official ZIP is also not a one-click move into Claude, Gemini, or Grok. It is a copy of your ChatGPT account data, delivered by OpenAI.

Before you decide a chat is lost

A chat missing from the sidebar is often still in your account. OpenAI's article Deleting and archiving chats in ChatGPT lists these checks:

  • Make sure you're signed in to the right account and workspace, with the same email address or single sign-on method you used before.
  • If you locked the chat, open Locked chats from the sidebar (or from Projects under Explore on mobile) and unlock it with your PIN. We explain this in ChatGPT locked chats.
  • Use Search. Archived chats stay searchable.
  • Open Settings, Data controls, then Archived chats (or View archived chats, depending on your device), and unarchive it.
  • Refresh ChatGPT, or sign out and back in, and check OpenAI Status for an active incident.

What can't come back is a deleted chat. According to OpenAI's chat retention article, a deleted chat disappears from your account view immediately, is scheduled for permanent deletion within 30 days, and can't be restored in ChatGPT or through OpenAI Support. Delete all chats in Data controls can't be undone either. Archive all chats is the safe alternative: archived chats keep the same retention as any saved chat. A temporary chat never enters your history unless you save it; see ChatGPT temporary chat.

Import the official ZIP into ConvoShelf to search it

After you download OpenAI's ZIP, open ConvoShelf's options (from the toolbar icon) and choose that file under Import. ConvoShelf reads the conversations.json file inside it (or the numbered conversations-000.json parts) on your computer. Nothing is uploaded. This is what version 1.2.0 does (live on Chrome, Firefox and Edge):

  • Importing and searching imported chats are free, and free and Pro get the same results (up to 50 per search).
  • ConvoShelf keeps up to 20,000 imported chats. A very long imported chat keeps its most recent 400,000 characters.
  • Imported chats show up in the same search list as chats you have opened, with an Imported badge until you open them once.
  • You can pin an imported chat or add it to a folder from the search results.
  • An import is all or nothing: Cancel, a damaged file or a closed tab leaves your library as it was. Importing the same export again does not add chats twice.
  • Official exports from Claude, Gemini or Grok cannot be imported.

Saving one open chat as Markdown or PDF

If you want a file of the chat on your screen, without waiting for the ZIP, ConvoShelf can do that on the computer where the chat is open:

  • Open the chat, and scroll to the top so every message has loaded.
  • Press Export in the ConvoShelf panel, and pick Markdown or PDF. PDF opens Chrome's print dialog; choose Save as PDF.
  • The free plan covers that single-chat export, along with unlimited top-level folders. Pro is $4.99, paid once, with no subscription, and adds JSON export, bulk export of a folder or every chat, and subfolders.
  • There is no ConvoShelf account. The chats stay in your browser. The same extension also works on Claude, Gemini, and Grok. We have a separate guide on backing up Claude chats and one on organizing ChatGPT chats into folders.

Use the official OpenAI export when you want a complete copy of your ChatGPT history. Import that ZIP into ConvoShelf when you want to search those chats. Use the local Markdown or PDF export when you want a file of one open chat now. If your ChatGPT plan is changing, we also wrote about exporting chats around a plan change.

FAQ

How do I export one ChatGPT conversation to Markdown or PDF?

Open the chat with ConvoShelf installed, scroll to the top so every message has loaded, press Export in the ConvoShelf panel and pick Markdown or PDF (PDF opens Chrome's print dialog; choose Save as PDF). Single-chat export is on the free plan. OpenAI's own export sends your whole history as one ZIP instead of one chat.

How do I keep ChatGPT chats from getting lost?

Request OpenAI's export from Settings, Data controls, Export data, and check the ZIP has the chats you need before you delete chats or move to a work workspace. Archive chats you want out of the sidebar instead of deleting them, because a deleted chat can't be restored. For a chat that matters, save a file of it as well.

How do I export my ChatGPT conversation history?

On the web, sign in to ChatGPT, open your profile menu, then Settings, then Data controls. Under Export data select Export, then Confirm export. OpenAI emails or texts a link when the ZIP is ready, which can take up to 7 days, and the link expires 24 hours after you receive it. Download it while signed in to the same account.

Who can use ChatGPT's official data export?

OpenAI's Help Center, read on 21 November 2026, says the export in settings is available for ChatGPT Free, Go, Plus and Pro, and eligible ChatGPT Edu workspaces. It is not available in ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or ChatGPT for Healthcare workspaces; OpenAI tells those users to contact their workspace owner. If you can't sign in, OpenAI points you to its Privacy Portal.

Can I get back a ChatGPT chat I deleted?

No. OpenAI says a deleted chat is removed from your account view immediately, scheduled for permanent deletion within 30 days, and can't be restored in ChatGPT or through OpenAI Support. An export also can't restore it. Archived chats are different: they stay in your account and you can unarchive them.

Can I import the official ChatGPT ZIP into ConvoShelf?

Yes, in version 1.2.0 (live on Chrome, Firefox and Edge). Open ConvoShelf's options and choose the ZIP under Import. ConvoShelf reads it on your computer, nothing is uploaded, and those chats become searchable. Importing is free, with a cap of 20,000 imported chats. It does not put chats back into ChatGPT.
